# 6. Convergence of a spectral sequence

## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. Tau Ceti has the spectral sequence of a filtered cochain complex in any abelian category, in the classical decreasing `E₁` indexing, with its `E∞` objects, the abutment `H^n(K)` with its image filtration, and strong convergence for finite filtrations (`CochainComplex.filtrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoGradedOfFinite`). That is Proposition 6.3, which is what [2020, 191(3), no. 4] uses. For complexes of modules it also has convergence for exhaustive filtrations and for complete exhaustive filtrations with degreewise bounded pages (Weibel 5.5.10, 5.5.11). The general notions of Definition 6.2 (weak, exhaustive/separated and strong convergence of an arbitrary spectral sequence to a filtered target, and convergence in a range of degrees, 6.2(5)) are not in code; open PR #2302 (SpectralSequencesAndConvergence) specifies them exactly (§3.2 convergence structures in a set `I` of total degrees, §4.4 convergence in a range of degrees).

## Mathlib
- Has: `CategoryTheory.SpectralSequence C c r₀` (pages and homology isos, `Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Basic.lean`), spectral objects and their spectral sequences (`Algebra/Homology/SpectralObject/*`, `HasSpectralSequence.lean`, first-quadrant `E₂` recipes).
- Missing: `E∞`, abutment filtrations, any convergence notion.

## Tau Ceti code
- `Abelian.SpectralObject.abutment`, `abutmentFiltration`, `abutmentFiltrationι`, `EInftyIsoCokernel` (`Algebra/Homology/SpectralObject/Abutment.lean`); stabilization `spectralSequencePageXIsoEInfty` (`SpectralObject/Stabilization.lean`).
- Filtered complexes: `CochainComplex.spectralSequence`, `filtrationAbutmentIso` (abutment = `H^n(K)`), `toFiltrationAbutmentFiltration` (image filtration), `isZero_filtrationAbutmentFiltration`, `isIso_filtrationAbutmentFiltrationι`, `filtrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoGradedOfFinite` (`Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Filtered/Convergence.lean`); `filtrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoEInftyOfFinite` (`.../Filtered/Stabilization.lean`).
- Infinite filtrations of complexes of modules: `CochainComplex.filtrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoGradedOfExhaustive`, `isIso_homologyMap_of_isIso_spectralSequence_page_of_exhaustive` (`.../Filtered/Exhaustive.lean`); for complete exhaustive filtrations with degreewise bounded pages (Weibel's Complete Convergence and Comparison Theorems 5.5.10, 5.5.11) `isZero_filtrationAbutmentFiltration_of_isCompleteFiltration`, `filtrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoGradedOfComplete`, `decreasingFiltrationSpectralSequencePageXIsoGradedOfComplete`, `isIso_homologyMap_of_isIso_spectralSequence_page_of_complete` (`.../Filtered/Complete.lean`). Weak convergence of sequences that are not degreewise bounded is not treated.
- Spectral sequences modulo a Serre class: `SpectralSequence.prop_page_X_of_le`, `prop_page_X_iff_of_forall`, `Hom.isoModSerre_hom_f_of_le` (`.../SpectralSequence/SerreClass.lean`).
- Decreasing filtrations, `E₁^{p,q} ≅ H^{p+q}(F^p/F^{p+1})`: `CochainComplex.decreasingFiltrationSpectralSequence`, `decreasingFiltrationSpectralSequenceFirstPageXIso` (`.../Filtered/Indexing.lean`).
- Degeneration: `SpectralSequence.page_d_eq_zero_of_le`, `pageXIsoOfLEOfIsZero` (`.../SpectralSequence/Degeneration.lean`); bicomplex and Čech spectral sequences built on these.

## Merged roadmaps
- No merged roadmap specifies an abstract convergence predicate. Consumers of the filtered-complex sequence: DerivedModuleAlgebra (§S.1 first-quadrant double complex, "convergent"), TopologicalSheafCohomology §1.4, SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.2 (Leray, Čech), ComplexKahlerGeometry K8 (Frölicher), AlgebraicTopology (Serre).

## Open roadmap PRs
- PR #2302 "SpectralSequencesAndConvergence: roadmap": Layer 1 (cycle and boundary towers of an arbitrary spectral sequence in Mathlib's carrier, stabilisation, the limit page, §§1.1–1.4), Layer 2 (bidegree assignments, boundedness, §§2.1–2.2), Layer 3 (§3.1 decreasing filtrations; §3.2 weak convergence, convergence and strong convergence in a set `I ⊆ ℤ` of total degrees to a filtered abutment `H : ℤ → FilteredObject C ℤᵒᵖ`, the three notions of Definition 6.2(1)–(3); §3.4 finite filtrations, which gives 6.2(4); §3.5 the comparison theorem), Layer 4 (§4.4 `HasConvergenceBounds` and strong convergence in degrees `I` for spectral objects, Definition 6.2(5); §4.5 Mathlib's quadrant spectral sequences converge), Layer 5 (filtered complexes: §5.3 convergence under cohomological boundedness, §5.4 exhaustive filtrations bounded below), Layers 6–7 (exact couples, conditional convergence, Boardman's theorems). It consumes the Tau Ceti filtered-complex API above.
- PR #2307 "SpectralSequencesAndConvergencePartII: roadmap": double complexes and their two spectral sequences with convergence (§1.3), Cartan–Eilenberg resolutions and hypercohomology spectral sequences, composite-functor and Künneth spectral sequences, on PR #2302's convergence structures.
- Many other PRs consume spectral sequences without defining convergence.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Spectral sequences, spectral objects: Mathlib.
2. `E∞`, abutment, image filtration, finite-filtration strong convergence, decreasing `E₁` indexing, and for module complexes exhaustive and complete-filtration convergence: Tau Ceti.
3. Limit page of an arbitrary spectral sequence and the convergence structures of Definition 6.2(1)–(4): PR #2302 §§1.4, 3.2, 3.4.
4. Convergence in a range of total degrees (6.2(5)): PR #2302 §4.4.
5. The paper's coefficient category (continuous ℓ-adic Galois representations, abelian): see the étale-cohomology roadmaps; outside this definition.

## Library-scale gap
Small, and specified by PR #2302: convergence structures on a spectral sequence with a filtered abutment (Weibel, *An Introduction to Homological Algebra*, §5.2; Boardman, *Conditionally convergent spectral sequences*; Stacks, Tag 012K), with the filtered-complex sequence from Tau Ceti as the first instance. What remains is review and merging of PR #2302, then implementation on top of Tau Ceti's `Algebra/Homology/SpectralSequence/Filtered/` code.
