## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
The Mumford–Tate group of a finite-dimensional rational Hodge structure is target §P.3a of merged [ShimuraVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraVarieties/README.md) (README l.189): `MT(V) ⊂ GL(V)` as the subgroup of the supremum `I_h` of the Hopf ideals of `O(GL(V))` killed by `h*` (`TauCeti.HopfIdeal.sSup_toIdeal`), with geometric connectedness, the sub-Hodge-structure criterion, the scalars in nonzero pure weight and reductivity for polarizable `V` (`TauCeti.Hodge.exists_isCompl_of_isPolarizable`). It sits on §P.3, the dictionary between Hodge structures and homomorphisms `h : S → GL(V)_ℝ` (l.177). Open PR #1038 §1.2 `mumfordTateGroup` specifies the same construction. The Deligne torus, Hopf-ideal joins and polarizable Hodge structures are in Tau Ceti; `h` and `MT(V)` are not. Uses #12 for `MT(A)`.

## Merged roadmaps
- [ShimuraVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraVarieties/README.md) (merged PR #933):
  - §P.3 "Hodge filtrations and reflex fields" (README l.177): the representation–Hodge dictionary, `h_C(z₁,z₂)` acting on type `(p,q)` by `z₁^(−p)z₂^(−q)` and `μ_h(z) = h_C(z,1)`, with conjugation, tensor, dual and Tate compatibilities from the split `S`-characters;
  - §P.3a "Mumford–Tate groups of rational Hodge structures" (l.189): for `h : S → GL(V)_ℝ` with rational weight (mixed weights allowed), `MT(V)` is the smallest closed `ℚ`-subgroup through whose real extension `h` factors, built as the subgroup of the Hopf ideal `I_h = sSup` of the Hopf ideals killed by `h*` (finitely generated since `O(GL(V))` is noetherian), with transport along isomorphisms; geometric connectedness without polarization; a rational subspace is a sub-Hodge structure iff it is `MT(V)`-stable; the weight cocharacter lies in `MT(V)` and, for `V ≠ 0` pure of weight `n ≠ 0`, so do the scalars; reductivity for polarizable `V`. Checks include `ℚ(1)`, `ℚ(0) ⊕ ℚ(1)`, `ℚ(ζ₅)` (where `ker h*` is not a Hopf ideal), elliptic curves with and without CM, and a non-polarizable complex torus with non-reductive `MT`. Not implemented.
  - §7.0i.1 (l.2029) specializes it to `H¹` of an abelian variety over a number field and proves `MT` is the fixer of finitely many untwisted rational Hodge tensors (Def. #89).
- Prerequisites in merged roadmaps:

## Prerequisite chain
1. Pure rational HS, with tensor, dual, Tate twist and polarizable splitting (`exists_isCompl_of_isPolarizable`). In Tau Ceti.
2. `O(GL_V)` as a Hopf algebra, joins of Hopf ideals (`HopfIdeal.sSup_toIdeal`), identity components, unipotent radical. In Tau Ceti.
3. Deligne torus `S = Res_{ℂ/ℝ}𝔾_m` as an algebraic group, with `h: S → GL_{V,ℝ}` and `μ_h`. `S`, its splitting over `ℂ`, `μ` and the characters are in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.DeligneTorus`, merged ReductiveGroupsPartII §RG2.0a.3); the HS-to-`h` dictionary is target §P.3 of merged ShimuraVarieties (not started).
4. `MT(V)` with connectedness, the stable-subspace criterion, scalars and reductivity: target §P.3a of merged ShimuraVarieties (not started); also PR #1038 §§1.2–1.4.
5. `MT(A) := MT(V_A)`. Needs #12 (merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §§A5.1–A5.5); for `A` over a number field, merged ShimuraVarieties §7.0i.1. Definition #23 (Hodge-generic abelian varieties) builds on this, cf. PR #1038 §1.5.
6. The Tannakian description is unplanned and not part of the definition.
