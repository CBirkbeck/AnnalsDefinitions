## Status
ROADMAP-PR
Open PR #2425 (BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices) specifies the whole definition for every lattice, arithmetic or not, with or without torsion: `PU(1,n)` as the full biholomorphism group of the ball (§2.1), lattices in `PU(1,n)` (§5.1), finite generation and Selberg's lemma (§§6.2–6.4), the analytic quotient `Γ\𝔹ⁿ` as a normal complex space (§7.1), Mok's projective minimal compactification `S*_Γ` with finite boundary (§§9.3–9.5, Theorem 15.4), uniqueness of the algebraic model (§10.4, Proposition 15.5) and the ball quotient `(S_Γ, φ_Γ)` with its uniformizing map (§10.4, Definition 15.6), and the comparison with Baily–Borel for arithmetic `Γ` (§11.4). Its inputs are merged: the ball, `PU(1,n)` and the Kähler form `ω_X` in [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) §5.7, lattices in VonNeumannAlgebras Layer 9 and [AdelicAlgebraicGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AdelicAlgebraicGroups/README.md) AA.2.3, AA.3.4, analytification and GAGA in [ComplexComparisonPartII](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ComplexComparisonPartII/README.md); the arithmetic theory it consumes is merged [ShimuraVarieties](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/ShimuraVarieties/README.md) (Baily–Borel §§2.5–2.7, Borel extension §3.1).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Lie groups and Haar measure. In Mathlib.
2. `U(1,n)`, `PU(1,n)` and the ball `𝔹^n ⊂ ℙ^n` with its transitive holomorphic action and invariant Kähler form `ω_X`: merged RealSemisimpleLieGroups §5.7. Full biholomorphism group (H. Cartan): PR #2425 §2.1.
3. Lattice in a real Lie group (discrete, finite covolume; 15.2): merged VonNeumannAlgebras Layer 9 and AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.2.3, AA.3.4 (not implemented), specialised to `PU(1,n)` in PR #2425 §5.1; Tau Ceti has the `n = 1` case (`IsCofinite`). Finite generation and Selberg's lemma (15.3): PR #2425 §§6.2–6.4.
4. Normal complex-space quotient `Γ\𝔹^n` (Cartan): PR #2425 §7.1, on merged ComplexComparisonPartII C-1.1–C-1.2.
5. Analytification of quasi-projective varieties and GAGA: merged ComplexComparisonPartII C-1.1, C1.9, C4.1–C4.4.
6. Existence of `S*_Γ` (Theorem 15.4) for every lattice: PR #2425 §§8.2, 9.3–9.5 (Mok); arithmetic case also through merged ShimuraVarieties §2.7, compared in PR #2425 §11.4.
7. Uniqueness (Proposition 15.5) and the ball quotient `(S_Γ, φ)` (Definition 15.6): PR #2425 §§10.2–10.4.
