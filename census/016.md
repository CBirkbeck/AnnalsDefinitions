## Status
ROADMAP-PR
Open PR #1996 Layer 2 specifies Vistoli's rational Chow groups of separated DM stacks, including the `1/|G|` degree of `BG`; open PR #2446 (VirtualFundamentalClassPartII) extends them to integral Chow groups of non-separated DM stacks and states the degree formula `∫[𝒢_x] = [κ(x):k]/|Aut(x̄)|` of a residual gerbe at a closed point (§5.4). Merged roadmaps cover only the prerequisites: DM stacks (SSF §1.19), coarse spaces (SSF §1.23), Chow groups of schemes (SSF §5.1–5.2), and residual gerbes `𝒵_x`, residue fields `κ(x)` and automorphism groups `G_x` of points of algebraic stacks (StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§1.3, 3.3, 5.3). Merged EquivariantIntersectionTheory explicitly excludes non-quotient stacks.

Algebraic spaces: the §1.3 predicates with `isAlgebraicSpace_of_etale_cover` (`AlgebraicSpace/EtaleCover.lean`) and `IsAlgebraicSpace.diagonal_locallyQuasiFinite` (`AlgebraicSpace/Diagonal.lean`), the category `AlgSpace` (`AlgebraicSpace/Category.lean`), and étale equivalence relations `Spaces.EtaleEquivRel` with `refl`, `symm`, `trans`, `of_isKernelPair` (`AlgebraicSpace/EtaleEquivRel.lean`, SSF §1.5 in part).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Cycles, rational equivalence, Chow groups and degree on schemes. In Mathlib (`AlgebraicCycle`) and Tau Ceti (`Chow`).
2. Proper pushforward on schemes and algebraic spaces. Merged SSF §5.2, partial; algebraic spaces unplanned in merged roadmaps for Chow (PR #2446 §2.3 compares with the Stacks Project's Chow groups of spaces).
3. Algebraic spaces and DM stacks, `|M|`, residual gerbes. Target in merged SSF §§1.3–1.20 (implemented: §1.3, the core of the §1.4 category `AlgSpace`, §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel`, and §1.18's `StackInGroupoids` carrier); residual gerbes, `κ(x)` and `G_x` in merged StackPointsAndResidualGerbes §§1.3, 3.3, 5.3 (not implemented; see #29).
4. Coarse moduli space (Keel–Mori). Target in merged SSF §1.23 (unimplemented).
5. `A_*(M)_ℚ` for DM stacks, and `deg` on proper `M`. Target in PR #1996 Layer 2; the residual-gerbe degree formula in PR #2446 §5.4.
6. Virtual class `[M]^vir`, needed by the paper. PR #1996 Layers 3–7 and PR #2446 Layer 6; this is definition #27 (perfect obstruction theories and virtual classes).
