## Open roadmap PRs
- #1488 "EnriquesSurfacesAndIntegralNonexistence":
  - §2.1: "An Enriques family over a scheme S is an algebraic space f:Y→S which is flat, proper and finitely presented, with every geometric fibre a geometric Enriques surface. Its isomorphisms and pullbacks form the fppf stack M_Enr." API: pullback, isomorphisms, smoothness; the stack property of `M_Enr` is not among them. *Needs:* WeilConjectures §7.12; SSF §§1.3–1.9 and 4.1; §§2.6 and 2.20; §§2.2–2.4, 2.11, 2.15 and 2.22. It does not say how a geometric fibre of an algebraic space is a scheme, which WeilConjectures §7.12's surface predicate requires.
  - §§2.2–2.13: relative Picard (Proposition 20.3(c)).
  - The headline target is nonexistence over `Spec ℤ`.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Schemes, flat, proper and f.p. morphisms, fppf topology. In Mathlib.
2. Algebraic spaces, their morphism properties and fppf descent. Target in merged SSF Layer 1 (§§1.2–1.9; §1.3, the core of §1.4 and §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel` implemented). Stacks in groupoids: SSF §1.18 (`StackInGroupoids` implemented).
3. Geometric fibres of an algebraic space over `S` being schemes (so that the Enriques predicate applies to them): stated nowhere; neither merged SSF Layer 1 nor PR #1488 contains it.
4. Enriques surface over an algebraically closed field (#19). PR #1208 §7.12.
5. Family of Enriques surfaces: target in PR #1488 §2.1. `M_Enr` as an fppf stack: named in #1488 §2.1 but not a target.

## Library-scale gap
Mostly planned (PR #1488) once merged SSF algebraic spaces and #19 exist. Unplanned: that geometric fibres of these algebraic-space families are schemes (or a version of the Enriques predicate for algebraic spaces over an algebraically closed field), and the stack property of `M_Enr` (descent for SSF §1.18). Reference: Schröer (the Annals paper) §5; Stacks Project, Algebraic Spaces.
