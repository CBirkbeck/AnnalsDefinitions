## Status
ROADMAP-PR
Open PR #1840 (PurityForFlatCohomology §0.2) specifies `RΓ_Z(X,F)` and `H^i_Z(X,F)` on the big fppf site, with the long exact sequence and excision. Merged SchemeAndStackFoundations §2.7 targets only the Zariski/module version. Tau Ceti has site-general relative cohomology `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.relativeH'` with the long exact sequence of a pair, which for the open immersion `U → X` is 21.1–21.2 (`H^i_Z = Ext^i(ℤ_{X,U}, F)`). It does not yet apply to the big fppf site: it takes sheaves valued in `AddCommGrpCat.{v}` for the morphism universe `v` of the site, which for `Scheme.{u}` is `u`, and sheafification of such sheaves on this large site is not available (it is for `AddCommGrpCat.{u+1}`). Open PR #2272 §2.0 defines fppf cohomology on the affine fppf site with that universe lift, overlapping #1840 §0.2.

## Mathlib
- `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.fppfTopology` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Fpqc.lean`).
- `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.H` (Ext from the constant sheaf, coefficients in `AddCommGrpCat.{w}` with `HasSheafify J AddCommGrpCat.{w}`) and `Sheaf.H'` (cohomology of an object `U`, Ext from the free sheaf on `U`, coefficients fixed in `AddCommGrpCat.{v}` for the morphism universe `v`), in `CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Basic.lean`. On `Scheme.fppfTopology` (`Scheme.{u}`, so `v = u`) the plus-construction sheafification instance needs colimits over covering sieves of size `u+1`; it exists for `AddCommGrpCat.{u+1}` and not for `AddCommGrpCat.{u}`. So `Sheaf.H` applies there only to universe-lifted sheaves, and `Sheaf.H'` not at all.
- `localCohomology` (`Algebra/Homology/LocalCohomology.lean`, colimit of Ext).
- No sections with support, and no cohomology with supports on sites.

## Tau Ceti code
- Fppf sheaves of group schemes and quotients (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/`); finite flat commutative group schemes over an affine base identified with finite locally free commutative affine group schemes (`toAffine`, `fromAffine`, `AlgebraicGeometry/GroupScheme/FiniteFlat/Affine.lean`).
- Punctured spectrum (`AlgebraicGeometry/PuncturedSpectrum/`).
- Zariski coherent cohomology (`AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/`).
- Relative cohomology on any site: `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.relativeH' F i n` = `Extⁿ(ℤ_{V,U}, F)` for `i : U ⟶ V`, with `relativeH'.zeroEquiv` (sections over `V` restricting to zero along `i`, the `Γ_Z` of 21.1), the long exact sequence of the pair for a monomorphism `i` (`relativeH'.exact_toH'_cohomologyPresheaf_map`, `exact_cohomologyPresheaf_map_δ`, `exact_δ_toH'`, the sequence of 21.2), the sequence of a triple and functoriality in the pair (`TauCeti/CategoryTheory/Sites/SheafCohomology/Relative.lean`). Like `Sheaf.H'`, it assumes `HasSheafify J AddCommGrpCat.{v}` for the morphism universe `v`, so it does not apply to the big fppf site of `Scheme.{u}`.
- Cohomology with closed supports for sheaves of abelian groups and of modules on a topological space: `H_Sⁿ(W, F)` as relative cohomology `CategoryTheory.Sheaf.supportedH`, sections supported in `S`, the support long exact sequence, the long exact sequence of a pair of supports and excision (`Topology/Sheaves/SupportedCohomology.lean` and `SupportedCohomology/`); local cohomology sheaves `𝓗_Sⁿ(F)` (`supportedHSheaf`), their coefficient long exact sequence, flasque vanishing and the edge comparison (`Topology/Sheaves/LocalCohomology/`).
- Degree-zero local cohomology of modules: `localCohomology.degreeZeroToModule` is injective with image the `J`-power torsion (`localCohomology.degreeZeroToModule_injective`, `range_degreeZeroToModule`; `Algebra/Homology/LocalCohomology/DegreeZero.lean`), SSF §2.7's `localCohomology_degreeZero`.
- Missing: a version of `relativeH'` on the big fppf site (it needs coefficients in a larger universe than the morphisms, or a small fppf site), excision and comparison (21.3) and the local case (21.4); the SSF §2.7 names `sectionsWithSupport`, `cohomologyWithSupport` for `O_X`-modules.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Big fppf site and abelian sheaves: in Mathlib. Cohomology on it: Mathlib `Sheaf.H` only for sheaves valued in `AddCommGrpCat.{u+1}`; `Sheaf.H'` and `Sheaf.relativeH'` need sheafification at the universe `u`, which is not available (PR #2272 §2.0 uses the universe lift on the affine site).
2. Commutative group scheme as an fppf sheaf. In Tau Ceti (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Fppf/`).
3. Zariski cohomology with supports and its comparison with `localCohomology`. Target in merged SSF §2.7; partial in Tau Ceti (topological-space supports, degree-zero comparison).
4. Fppf/étale/Zariski comparisons. Target in merged SSF §2.10 (partial: the étale-to-Nisnevich morphism of topoi only; no fppf comparison).
5. Fppf `RΓ_Z`, `H^i_Z`, and `H^i_𝔪(R,G)`. Target in PR #1840 §0.2; the site-general construction and its long exact sequence (21.1–21.2) are Tau Ceti `Sheaf.relativeH'`, not usable on the big fppf site (item 1).
