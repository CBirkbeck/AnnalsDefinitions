# 25. Nash blowup of a variety

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2413 (NashBlowups) specifies the definition: `nashBlowup` as the reduced closure of the smooth locus in the Grassmannian of rank-`d` quotients of `Ω_{X/k}` (§§1.2, 2.1), with the Nash cotangent bundle, properness and the modification property (§2.2), the universal property (§2.4), locality (§2.6), iterates (§2.7), the Gauss-map description (§3.1) and the blowup description (§3.2). Its main input, the Grassmannian `Gr_d(E)` of a quasi-coherent sheaf of finite type, is a target of merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (`coherentGrassmannian`), not implemented. The other ingredients are in code (Kähler sheaf, smooth locus, affine Grassmannian scheme, scheme-theoretic image).

Used by #26.

## Mathlib
- `Module.Grassmannian R M k` and `Module.Grassmannian.functor` (quotient convention, `Mathlib/RingTheory/Grassmannian.lean`); its TODO lists "Grassmannians for schemes and quasi-coherent sheaf of modules" and representability.
- Smooth locus: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Smooth.lean`), `Algebra.smoothLocus`.
- Scheme-theoretic image: `Scheme.Hom.image`, `Hom.toImage` (`AlgebraicGeometry/IdealSheaf/Subscheme.lean`).
- Kähler differentials of algebras `Ω[S⁄R]`; no sheaf of differentials on schemes.

## Tau Ceti code
- Sheaf of differentials: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.relativeDifferentials`, `universalDerivation` (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Differentials/Basic.lean`), quasi-coherence, locally free on smooth schemes (`FiniteLocallyFreeSheaf.relativeDifferentials`, `Differentials/LocallyFree.lean`).
- Modifications: `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.IsModification` (`AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Modification/`), the iso locus and centre of a morphism `Scheme.Hom.isoLocus`, `Scheme.Hom.centre` (`Morphisms/IsoLocus.lean`) with `IsModification.dense_isoLocus`, `IsModification.isNowhereDense_centre` (`Modification/Centre.lean`), strict transform under a blow-up (`Blowup/StrictTransform.lean`).
- Grassmannian scheme of a module over an affine base: `Module.Grassmannian.scheme R M k`, `toSpec`, charts (`TauCeti/AlgebraicGeometry/Grassmannian/Basic.lean`), points `schemeHomEquiv` (`Points.lean`), valuative criterion/properness for finite `M` (`Proper.lean`), universal quotient (`UniversalQuotient.lean`), base change `G(k, R' ⊗[R] M) ≅ Spec R' ×_{Spec R} G(k, M)` (`Module.Grassmannian.isPullback_baseChangeHom`, `BaseChange.lean`), which identifies the restriction of `G(k, M)` to a basic open with `G(k, M[1/f])`.
- So the affine case `X = Spec A` (Grass of `Ω_{A/k}` over `Spec A`, section over the smooth locus, scheme-theoretic image) is definable from existing code. No `nashBlowup` or `coherentGrassmannian` declaration exists. Missing for the global definition: Grassmannian of a quasi-coherent sheaf on a non-affine `X` (gluing of the affine construction along the base-change isomorphisms).

## Prerequisite chain
1. `Ω_{X/k}` as coherent sheaf: Tau Ceti (`Scheme.relativeDifferentials`).
2. Smooth locus `X_sm`, density for perfect `k`: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus`; PR #2413 §§0.3, 0.5.
3. Grassmannian of quotients of a module over an affine base: Tau Ceti (`Module.Grassmannian.scheme`).
4. `Gr_d(E)` for quasi-coherent finite-type `E` on a scheme, with base change and Plücker: merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3 (not implemented), on ModularCurves §0.7 (partial: affine base with base change only).
5. Section `σ: X_sm → Gr_d(Ω)` from the locally free rank-`d` quotient: follows from 4's universal property (PR #2413 §1.2).
6. Scheme-theoretic image and reduced structure: Mathlib `Scheme.Hom.image`, Tau Ceti `Scheme.Hom.isReduced_image`.
7. Nash blowup and iterates (25.4), locality (25.5(c)), Gauss-map description (25.5(d)): PR #2413 §§2.1–2.7, 3.1.

## Library-scale gap
Planned in PR #2413, on merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §1.3. The toric/logarithmic-Jacobian computation for toric varieties needed by the paper is left to a toric roadmap. References: Nobile, *Some properties of the Nash blowing-up*; González-Sprinberg; EGA I (1971) §9.7.
