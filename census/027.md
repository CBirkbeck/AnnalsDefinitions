# 27. Perfect obstruction theory and virtual fundamental class

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED
Merged [VirtualFundamentalClass](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/VirtualFundamentalClass/README.md) specifies Behrend–Fantechi: the intrinsic normal sheaf and cone (Layer 5, 27.3 absolute), obstruction theories with `H⁰` iso and `H⁻¹` epi and their closed-immersion criterion (§6.1, 27.4–27.5), perfect obstruction theories and virtual rank (§6.4), global two-term resolutions (§6.5), `[X,φ]^vir = 0^!_{F₁}[C(F•)] ∈ A_n(X)_ℚ` and its independence of the resolution (§§7.1–7.4, 27.6), the virtual degree (§7.7), and the relative cone and class over a pure-dimensional algebraic stack (§§9.1–9.2, 27.3–27.6 relative). Nothing is implemented. Flags: 27.7 (the Gromov–Witten obstruction theory) needs #24 and is only in open PR #1998 Layer 4; open PR #2446 drops the resolution property and separatedness (§6.2). The merged GromovWittenTheory and ImplicitAtlases roadmaps are the symplectic (Pardon) theory, not Behrend–Fantechi.

Depends on #16 (rational Chow groups of DM stacks) and #24 (for 27.7).

## Library-scale gap
Specified in merged VirtualFundamentalClass; the Gromov–Witten case (27.7) in PRs #1998 and #2446. The paper additionally uses torus-equivariant virtual localization (Graber–Pandharipande) and Kiem–Li cosection localization, which the boundaries of VirtualFundamentalClass and PR #2446 exclude; these would be a follow-on roadmap beside VirtualFundamentalClass. References: Behrend–Fantechi, *The intrinsic normal cone*; Behrend, *Gromov–Witten invariants in algebraic geometry*; Fulton, *Intersection theory*.
