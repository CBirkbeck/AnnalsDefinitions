# 27. Perfect obstruction theory and virtual fundamental class

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1996 (VirtualFundamentalClass) specifies exactly Behrend–Fantechi: intrinsic normal cone, obstruction theories, `[X,φ]^vir ∈ A_n(X)_ℚ` with global resolution, and the relative version; open PR #2446 (VirtualFundamentalClassPartII) drops the resolution property and separatedness (§6.2) and gives the obstruction theory of relative spaces of maps from prestable curves (Layer 9); open PR #1998 (GromovWittenTheory) Layer 4 specifies the Gromov–Witten obstruction theory and class (27.7). Nothing implemented. The merged GromovWittenTheory and ImplicitAtlases roadmaps are the symplectic (Pardon) theory, not Behrend–Fantechi.

Depends on #16 (rational Chow groups of DM stacks) and #24 (for 27.7).

- Algebraic cycles `AlgebraicGeometry/AlgebraicCycle/Basic.lean`; naive cotangent complex / `Algebra.Extension.Cotangent` (`RingTheory/Extension/Cotangent/`), Kähler differentials; derived categories of abelian categories (`CategoryTheory/Triangulated`, `DerivedCategory`). ## Open roadmap PRs
- #1996 "roadmap: construct the Behrend–Fantechi virtual fundamental class": Layer 0 stack dimension; Layer 1 normal cones; Layer 2 rational Chow theory and Gysin maps for DM stacks (#16); Layer 3 cotangent complexes and perfect complexes on stacks; Layer 4 cone stacks and `h¹/h⁰`; Layer 5 intrinsic normal sheaf and cone; Layer 6 obstruction theories (`H⁰` iso, `H⁻¹` epi; closed-immersion criterion = 27.5); Layer 7 construction and independence of `[X,φ]^vir` (end goal 3 is exactly 27.6's formula `0^!_{F₁}[C(F•)]`); Layer 9 relative version over a smooth Artin stack (27.3–27.4 relative case). Boundary: no particular moduli stack, no cosection/equivariant localization.
- #2446 "VirtualFundamentalClassPartII": Layers 1–4 integral and rational Chow theory of DM stacks with quasi-compact separated diagonal (see #16); §6.1 the comparison bundle of two global resolutions; §6.2 `virtualClass` `[X/𝔜, E]^vir := 0^!_{F_1}[C(F•)]` (27.6) without the resolution property or separatedness, independent of the resolution; §6.3 relative formulas over a base of pure dimension; Layer 7 relative obstruction classes and the relative characterisation of obstruction theories (27.3–27.5); Layer 8 base change and deformation invariance; Layer 9 `RelativeSpaceOfMaps` with the perfect relative obstruction theory `(Rπ_* F^*T_X)^∨ → L_{M/𝔅}`, global resolutions and virtual dimension (27.7's construction for any family of prestable curves). It consumes #1996.
- #1998 "roadmap: develop algebraic Gromov-Witten theory and quantum cohomology" Layer 4: `E = (Rπ_* f^*T_X)^∨ → L_{MbarMap/Mpre}`, global two-term resolution, `[MbarMap g n X β]^vir` with `vdim = (1−g)(d−3)+n+c₁(T_X)·β` (27.7).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Chow groups of schemes: Tau Ceti `Chow.ChowGroup` (partial: §5.1).
2. Normal cones, Gysin maps for schemes: SchemeAndStackFoundations §5.5 (merged, not implemented).
3. Algebraic/DM stacks, `QCoh`: SchemeAndStackFoundations §§1.18–1.22 (merged; only §1.18's `StackInGroupoids` is implemented).
4. Cotangent complex (rings, then schemes/stacks): DerivedModuleAlgebra §0.4 (merged) → PR #1996 Layer 3.
5. Rational Chow groups of DM stacks, Gysin maps (#16): PR #1996 Layer 2.
6. `h¹/h⁰`, intrinsic normal cone: PR #1996 Layers 4–5.
7. Perfect obstruction theory, virtual class: PR #1996 Layers 6–7, 9; without the resolution property, PR #2446 Layers 6–7.
8. GW obstruction theory on `M̄_{g,n}(X,β)`: PR #1998 Layer 4 (needs #24 from PR #1998 Layer 3); the general relative-maps case is PR #2446 Layer 9.

## Library-scale gap
Fully planned in PRs #1996, #2446 and #1998. The paper additionally uses torus-equivariant virtual localization (Graber–Pandharipande) and Kiem–Li cosection localization, which the boundaries of PRs #1996 and #2446 exclude; these would be a follow-on roadmap beside VirtualFundamentalClass. References: Behrend–Fantechi, *The intrinsic normal cone*; Behrend, *Gromov–Witten invariants in algebraic geometry*; Fulton, *Intersection theory*.
