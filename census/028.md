# 28. Points of an algebraic stack

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.20 specifies `Stacks.points X : TopCat` (field-valued objects modulo isomorphism after common extension, quotient topology from a smooth atlas, atlas independence, functoriality) and `automorphismGroup x`, the stabilizer of a point of a stack (§1.14's `stabilizerAt` is the analogue for groupoids in algebraic spaces). Not implemented (Layer 1 has §1.3, the core of the §1.4 category `AlgSpace`, §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel`, and §1.18's `StackInGroupoids` with its morphisms). The parts of 28.2–28.4 that §1.20 does not name (finite type and closed points, `π₀ 𝒳(Spec k) → |𝒳|`, open substacks ↔ open subsets) are specified in open PR #2429 (StackPointsAndResidualGerbes) §§0.3, 1.1–1.2.

Used by #29.

## Merged roadmaps
- SchemeAndStackFoundations Layer 1:
  - §1.7 points of an algebraic space (`|X|`); §1.14 `stabilizer`, `stabilizerAt` (field-valued point) for groupoids in algebraic spaces, not for stacks,
  - §1.18 `StackInGroupoids`, `yonedaEquiv` (`Hom(h_T, X) ≃ X(T)`, gives `X(k)` as a groupoid, 28.3(a)),
  - §1.19 `IsAlgebraicStack`, `IsDeligneMumford`,
  - §1.20 `inertia`, `automorphismGroup x` (28.3(b)), and **"Underlying topological spaces of algebraic stacks"**: `Stacks.points`, `.ofSpace`, `.ofScheme`, `.atlasIndependent`, `.map`, `.atlasOpenSurjective`, `.pullbackSurjective` (Stacks 04XG, 04XL), plus locally Noetherian and finite-type stacks.
  - In code: §1.3 in full, the core of §1.4 (`AlgSpace`, `toPresheaf`, `ofScheme`), §1.5's `EtaleEquivRel` and the §1.18 carrier `StackInGroupoids`; missing are §1.4's `AlgSpace.overEquiv`, §1.5's `quotientSheaf`, §§1.6–1.17, the rest of §1.18 (`ofSheaf`, `yonedaEquiv`, stackification, 2-fibre products) and §§1.19–1.23.
- Not named as targets in merged roadmaps: closed points, finite-type points and their density (28.4(b)), smooth points (28.3(c)), affine stabilizers, open substacks ↔ open subsets (28.2(c)). PR #2429 specifies all but smooth points.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #2429 "StackPointsAndResidualGerbes": §0.3 open, closed and locally closed substacks with "`𝒰 ↦ |𝒰|` is an inclusion-preserving bijection from open substacks to open subsets" (28.2(c)); §1.1 `Stacks.IsFiniteTypePoint`, density of finite type points in every closed subset (28.4(b)); §1.2 closed points are finite type, `Stacks.pointOfObject` `π₀ 𝒳(Spec k) → |𝒳|` with its fibres (28.4(a)); §1.3 the automorphism group algebraic space `G_x` of a field-valued point, §1.4 when it is a scheme (affine stabilizers). Smooth points (28.3(c)) are not named. It builds on SSF §§1.18–1.22.
- #1807, #1854 (SchemeAndStackFoundations edits) and #1831, #1202 carry the same §1.20 text; no new targets for points.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Algebraic spaces and their points: Tau Ceti `Spaces.IsAlgebraicSpace`, `Spaces.AlgSpace` (§1.3 done, §1.4 core); §1.7 (merged, not implemented).
2. Stacks in groupoids, 2-fibre products: §1.18 (merged; `StackInGroupoids` and its morphisms in Tau Ceti, `yonedaEquiv`, stackification and 2-fibre products not).
3. Algebraic stacks, smooth atlases: §1.19 (merged, not implemented).
4. `|X|` with topology, `automorphismGroup`: §1.20 (merged, not implemented).
5. Closed / finite-type points, `X(k)/≅ → |X|` (28.4), open substacks (28.2(c)), `G_x`: PR #2429 §§0.3, 1.1–1.4. Smooth points (28.3(c)): unplanned (small extension of §1.20).
