# 29. Residual gerbe at a point of an algebraic stack

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2429 (StackPointsAndResidualGerbes) specifies the definition and its theory: monomorphisms and closed substacks of algebraic stacks (Layer 0), gerbes over algebraic stacks (Layer 2), `Stacks.residualGerbe` `𝒵_x` with its characterisation and uniqueness (§3.3, 29.1–29.2), existence for finite type points, quasi-compact inertia and locally Noetherian stacks (§§4.1–4.4, 29.3(a)), the closed-immersion criterion at closed points (§4.5), `𝒵_x ≃ BG_x` at a rational point (§§6.3–6.4, 29.4), and the normal space `N_x` as a `G_x`-representation (§7.2, 29.5). Its prerequisites are merged targets: algebraic stacks, `|X|`, `BG` and `QCoh` on stacks in SchemeAndStackFoundations §§1.18–1.22, and site-level gerbes in AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §4.1; none is implemented beyond §1.18's `StackInGroupoids` carrier.

Depends on #28.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Points `|X|`, stabilizers `G_x = automorphismGroup x` (#28): SchemeAndStackFoundations §1.20 (merged, not implemented); `G_x` as a group algebraic space in PR #2429 §1.3.
2. Monomorphisms, reduced/locally Noetherian stacks, substacks: PR #2429 Layer 0, on SSF §§1.18–1.20.
3. `BG = [Spec k/G]`: SSF §1.21 (merged, not implemented).
4. Gerbes: site-level in merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometry §4.1 (not implemented); over algebraic stacks in PR #2429 Layer 2.
5. Residual gerbe `Z_x` (29.1–29.2), existence for quasi-separated / locally Noetherian stacks (29.3(a)): PR #2429 Layers 3–4.
6. `Z_x ≃ BG_x` at a `k`-point (29.4): PR #2429 §§6.3–6.4.
7. Infinitesimal neighbourhoods and normal space `N_x = (I/I²)^∨` as a `G_x`-representation (29.5): PR #2429 §§7.1–7.2, on SSF §1.22 `QCoh(BG)`.
