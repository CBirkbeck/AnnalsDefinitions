# 31. Totally geodesic subvariety of a ball quotient

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2425 (BallQuotientsAndComplexHyperbolicLattices) §12.1 defines totally geodesic subvarieties of `S_Γ` (Definition 31.4, with "every irreducible component of `π⁻¹(Z^an)`", equivalent to "some" by transitivity) and Γ-special sub-balls and subvarieties (31.5(b)); §12.2 proves Γ-special subvarieties are algebraic and totally geodesic, §12.3 the converse, and §3.3 the analytic-subset clause of Proposition 31.3. "Strict" and "maximal" are not named there; they are one-line order-theoretic definitions on top of §12.1. Setting 31.1 and the rest of Proposition 31.3 are merged targets in [RealSemisimpleLieGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RealSemisimpleLieGroups/README.md) §5.7 (on §5.5's local Lie-triple-system statement), total geodesicity via the second fundamental form (31.2) is merged RiemannianGeometry R5a, and analytic subsets with their singular loci and irreducible components are merged ComplexKahlerGeometry §3.4. The ball quotient `S_Γ` (#15) is a target of the same PR #2425 (§10.4).

Depends on #15 (ball quotients) and #68 (arithmetic lattices, needed only for the papers' theorems).
