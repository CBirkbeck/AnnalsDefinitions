# 34. Neumann eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of the Laplacian on a bounded domain

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR 1614 "PDE Mixed Boundary Conditions" (Lane G, item 32) specifies the Neumann (Γ = ∅) spectrum of −Δ on a bounded connected Lipschitz domain via the form ∫∇u·∇v on H¹(Ω); merged PDE has only the Dirichlet spectrum, which is implemented. PR 1614 is written against the older lane layout of the PDE roadmap (Lanes A–G), while merged PDE is organized in Layers 1–9 with sections `n.m`, so Lane G needs rewriting into that structure, not only a rebase. Its connectedness hypothesis misses part of Theorem 34.2, where `dim E_0` is the number of connected components of Ω. Tau Ceti's `IsCoercive.formOperator` applied to `W^{1,2}(Ω)` with the Rellich value map gives the Neumann operator; this application is not in code.

## Mathlib
- Has: Laplacian `InnerProductSpace.instLaplacian`, `LinearPMap.IsSelfAdjoint`, compact operators and the spectral theorem for compact self-adjoint operators, whole-space Bessel potential spaces `BesselPotentialSpace` (`H^{s,p}(E,F)`).
- Missing: H¹(Ω) on a domain, Neumann form, Neumann eigenvalues.

- Interior regularity (PDE §5.1): interior `H²`/`Hᵏ` estimates and the C^∞ bootstrap, `UniformlyEllipticOn.exists_value_eq_value_restrictL_of_contDiffOn` (`Analysis/PDE/Regularity/HigherOrder.lean`), `UniformlyEllipticOn.exists_contDiffOn_ae_eq` (`Analysis/PDE/Regularity/Smooth.lean`); Lipschitz-coefficient `H²` via `exists_lowerOrder_eq_restrictL_of_locallyLipschitzOn`.
- The abstract operator of a symmetric form coercive after a shift along `J : V → H`: `IsCoercive.formOperator` with its graph and domain (`mem_graph_formOperator_iff`, `mem_domain_formOperator_iff`), shift independence `formOperator_eq`, `isSelfAdjoint_formOperator`, the lower bound, `hasCompactResolvent_formOperator` for compact `J` and the eigenbasis `exists_hilbertBasis_formOperator` (`Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Variational/FormOperator.lean`). With `V = W^{1,2}(Ω)`, `J` the Rellich value map and `Q(u, v) = ∫∇u·∇v`, this is the Neumann realization with its eigenbasis. `TauCeti.W1p mu Ω 2` is an `InnerProductSpace ℝ` by instance search: `W1p` is an `abbrev` for the closed submodule `w1pSubmodule` of the `L²` jet space `Sobolev1JetLp` (`Analysis/Sobolev/W1p/Basic.lean`), and `#synth InnerProductSpace ℝ (W1p mu Omega 2)` returns `Submodule.innerProductSpace`; with `CompleteSpace` it is a Hilbert space. The specialization of `formOperator` to it is not in code.
- Missing: Neumann solution operator / eigenvalues (the only "Neumann" in `Analysis/PDE` and `Analysis/Sobolev` is the Neumann series bound in `Analysis/PDE/Parabolic/Holder/`).

## Merged roadmaps
- PDE §4.4 (D19) "The Dirichlet spectrum": Dirichlet only; implemented. PDE §1.6 (Lipschitz domains, trace, Rellich): the needed compactness H¹(Ω) ↪ L²(Ω); implemented in large part.
- `TauCetiRoadmap/VariationalMethods/` ([README](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/VariationalMethods/README.md)): compact form pairs (§3.1), successive minima and Courant–Fischer on the form domain `CompactFormPair.eigenvalue_eq_rayleighMinMax` (§§4.1–4.4, the min–max formula of Thm 34.2 and 34.4(c)), the represented operator and the differential-energy adapter `D₀†D₀` (§§5.1–5.3). Abstract; it does not mention Neumann conditions. Not implemented (no `CompactFormPair` in Tau Ceti).
- No merged roadmap specifies Neumann eigenvalues.

## Open roadmap PRs
- PR 1614 "PDE Mixed Boundary Conditions", Lane G item 32 "Mixed and Neumann spectra": resolvent (−Δ_Γ+1)⁻¹ via Lax–Milgram on H¹_Γ(Ω), compact by Lipschitz Rellich, eigenvalues λ_k with orthonormal eigenbasis; for Γ = ∅ first eigenvalue 0 with eigenspace the constants. Item 31 gives the pure Neumann problem. It restricts to connected Ω; Theorem 34.2 allows disconnected Ω and states `dim E_0` = number of connected components, which needs the locally constant functions on each component, not the connected case alone. The PR uses the old Lane A–G layout of the PDE roadmap and needs rewriting into merged PDE's Layer structure (Layer 4 "linear elliptic existence by the energy method" holds the Dirichlet spectrum §4.4).
- Not specified anywhere: Prop 34.3 regularity (real analyticity inside, C^∞ up to smooth boundary, polygon corner behaviour, classical ⇔ weak equivalence), which the two papers use to pass between classical and weak formulations.

## Prerequisite chain
1. H¹(Ω), Lipschitz domains, Rellich, Poincaré–Wirtinger: Tau Ceti (PDE §1.1, §1.5, §1.6 targets, implemented).
2. Compact self-adjoint spectral theorem / Courant–Fischer: Mathlib + Tau Ceti.
3. Neumann form, resolvent, eigenvalue sequence μ_k: target PR 1614 item 32 for connected Ω (not implemented); the abstract operator is Tau Ceti `IsCoercive.formOperator` on the Hilbert space `W1p mu Ω 2`. Eigenspace of 0 = locally constant functions on a disconnected Ω: not specified.
4. Min–max on the form domain: target of merged VariationalMethods §4.4, not implemented.
5. Interior regularity (Δu = μu classically, analytic): PDE §5.1 (E20) interior H^k estimates and C^∞ bootstrap (merged; the interior part is implemented, only the global boundary versions of Layer 5 remain); analyticity unplanned.
6. Boundary regularity on C^∞ domains and on polygons, and the classical–weak equivalence (Prop 34.3(ii),(iii)): unplanned (PDE Layer 5 lists "global boundary estimates" as remaining but for Dirichlet; Neumann boundary regularity and corner analysis not specified).

## Library-scale gap
The definition itself is covered by PR 1614 up to the disconnected case of `E_0`, once its Lane G is rewritten into merged PDE's layers. What the papers additionally need is Neumann boundary regularity: global H² / C^∞ regularity up to a smooth boundary for the Neumann problem and corner regularity on polygons (Grisvard, *Elliptic Problems in Nonsmooth Domains*, Ch. 4–5; Evans §6.3 for the smooth case). This would naturally extend PDE Lane E / PR 1614's Lane G.
