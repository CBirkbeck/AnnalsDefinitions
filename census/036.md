1. C*-algebras, positive functionals, CP maps, `CStarMatrix`, `Matrix.trace`: in Mathlib.
2. Tracial state: implicit in Tau Ceti (`isKMSState_zero_iff`, `kmsStates`); `IsTracialState`, `T(A)`, faithfulness: AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras §§1.1–1.2 (merged, not implemented).
3. Normalised trace and 2-norm on `M_n`: AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras §1.5 (merged, not implemented; on Tau Ceti's Frobenius inner product).
4. c.p.c. maps into `M_n`: Mathlib `CompletelyPositiveMap` plus contractivity; Kadison–Schwarz in Tau Ceti; unital extension and multiplicative domain CStarAlgebras C4.1 (partly in code); AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras Layer 2 (needs C4.2 Stinespring for §2.2).
5. Amenable trace (Def 36.3): AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras §3.1 (merged, not implemented; needs only 2 and 3).
6. Equivalences (Prop 36.4): §3.2 (sequences, u.c.p. maps), §3.4 (pullback), §§4.1–4.4 (hypertraces; need C4.1 multiplicative domain, C5.1–C5.2, V13a), §§5.3–5.4 (nuclear and exact cases; need C6.1, C6.5, C7.1, V13a); merged, not implemented.
7. Application (Schafhauser): simple unital AF algebras (CStarClassification CL2; inductive limits CStarAlgebras C5.4, not in code), AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras §7.2 and §8.5; UCT and KK-theory (OperatorKTheory, not examined here).

## Library-scale gap
Planned in full by AmenableTracesOnCStarAlgebras: the gap is implementation. The definition itself (§3.1) waits only on §§1.1–1.2 and §1.5, whose inputs are in place in Tau Ceti's KMS-state and Frobenius API. Proposition 36.4 then waits on the CStarAlgebras layers it consumes (the rest of C4.1, C4.2, C5.1–C5.2, C6, C7.1, C8), of which C2.1 (unital), the C*-level part of C3.2, Kadison–Schwarz from C4.1 and the bounded-product part of C5.4 are in code, and on VonNeumannAlgebras Layer 13.
