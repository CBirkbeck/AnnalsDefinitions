## Prerequisite chain
1. Eigenvalues of Hermitian matrices/self-adjoint maps, `esymm`, symmetric cones: in Mathlib; eigenvalue inequalities in Tau Ceti.
2. Relative eigenvalue vector, spectral functions, admissible pairs and structural condition: PR #2440 Layers 1–2.
3. Compact complex manifold, `T^{1,0}`, real `(1,1)`-forms: ComplexKahlerGeometry K1 (merged, partial).
4. `i∂∂̄φ` for `φ ∈ C²(X)`: in Tau Ceti (`TauCeti.manifoldLeviForm`); its comparison with K2's `i∂∂̄` and continuity: PR #2440 §3.1.
5. Hermitian metric `ω_X`: a `TauCeti.SmoothTwoForm.Compatible` two-form, as #2440's Conventions use it.
6. Eigenvalue function `λ_ω[χ_φ]` (38.1), the equation and its normalisation (38.3): PR #2440 §§3.1–3.3.
7. For the theorem: Yau's theorem, exponential integrability and the `L^∞` estimates: PR #2440 Layers 5–8, on ComplexKahlerGeometry K4, K5a, K6.0 (merged, not implemented) and PDE §§3.2, 5.6. Pluripotential theory is outside #2440.
