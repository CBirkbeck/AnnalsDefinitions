## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged DiracScatteringAndNonlinearFourierTransform specifies it: the scattering limit `a(∞,·)`, `b(∞,·)`, `ρ(∞,·) = b(∞,·)/a(∞,·)` locally uniformly on `ℂ₊` for square-integrable potentials (§5.1, Prop 43.1 on `ℂ₊`), the non-linear Fourier transform `q⌃ = ρ(∞,·)` with truncations `(q_T)⌃ = b(T,·)/a(T,·)` (§5.2, Def 43.2(b)), its boundary function on `ℝ` (§§5.2–5.3), and the convergence of truncations in `L²(ℝ)` and in `Π`-measure, with `a(T,·) → a(∞,·)`, `b(T,·) → b(∞,·)` in `Π`-measure (§5.5, Prop 43.1 on `ℝ`, Def 43.2(a)). §9.6 states the paper's pointwise convergence theorem only at resonance-free points (its conditional form); that almost every point is resonance-free is not a target, and the roadmap (README l.136–149) cites an error report (Mnatsakanyan 2022) against an earlier arXiv version of the paper's proof and Denisov 2026, which poses the almost-everywhere convergence for potentials on `ℝ` truncated to `(−T, T)` as a question. It rests on the scattering functions of #44 (Layer 4) and on the half-plane function theory of its Layer 2, whose §2.2 uses merged AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.5. Nothing is implemented.

1. `L²(ℝ₊)`, convergence in measure, finite measure `Π`: in Mathlib.
2. Carathéodory linear ODE: in Tau Ceti for bounded coefficients only; `L¹_loc` with entire parameter dependence: target of merged DiracScattering Layer 1 (not started).
3. Scattering functions `a(t,z)`, `b(t,z)` and their properties (#44): target of merged DiracScattering Layers 3–4.
4. Truncated transform `b(T,·)/a(T,·)` (Def 43.2(b)): target of merged DiracScattering §5.2.
5. Bounded holomorphic functions, Fatou, outer functions on `ℂ₊`: target of merged DiracScattering Layer 2 (§2.2 on merged AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.5; not started).
6. Existence of scattering limits on `ℂ₊` and in `Π`-measure on ℝ (Prop 43.1): target of merged DiracScattering §5.1, §5.5.
7. Nonlinear Fourier transform `b(∞,·)/a(∞,·)` (Def 43.2(a)): target of merged DiracScattering §§5.2–5.5.
