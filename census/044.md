## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged DiracScatteringAndNonlinearFourierTransform specifies the definition for locally integrable complex potentials, the real case of 44 included: the linear Carathéodory theorem with `L¹_loc` coefficients and holomorphic dependence on a parameter (§§1.1–1.6, Prop 44.1), the Dirac system and its fundamental matrix (§3.1), the Hermite–Biehler functions `E`, `Ẽ` (§§3.2–3.3), and the scattering matrix `G_q` with `a = (G_q)₂₂`, `b = (G_q)₂₁` (§4.1, Def 44.3) with `det G = 1`, `G ∈ SU(1,1)` on `ℝ`, the dictionary `a = e^{itz}(E + iẼ)/2` (Prop 44.4) and the half-plane bound `|a| ≥ 1` (§4.3). Layers 1, 3 and 4 consume only Mathlib and Tau Ceti. Nothing is implemented; Tau Ceti has a Carathéodory linear ODE for essentially bounded coefficients only.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Lebesgue integration, `L¹_loc`, matrices, entire functions: in Mathlib.
2. Carathéodory linear ODE, bounded measurable coefficients on compact intervals: in Tau Ceti (`Caratheodory.lean`).
3. Extension to `L¹_loc` coefficients and entire dependence on a parameter (Prop 44.1): target of merged DiracScattering §§1.1–1.6 (not started; needs only Mathlib and Tau Ceti).
4. Real Dirac system, transfer matrix `M(t,z)`, Hermite–Biehler functions `E, Ẽ`: target of merged DiracScattering §§3.1–3.3 (on Layer 1).
5. Scattering functions `a, b` (Def 44.3) and Prop 44.4 (`det G = 1`, `|a| ≥ 1`, `SU(1,1)` on ℝ): target of merged DiracScattering §4.1, §4.3.
