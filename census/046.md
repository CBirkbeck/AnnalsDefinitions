## Status
ROADMAP-PR: open PR #2421 (SchattenClassesAndSchurMultipliers) specifies the definition in full: the Hilbert–Schmidt kernel isomorphism `L²(μ ⊗ μ) ≅ S₂(L²(μ))` for σ-finite `μ` (§2.1, Prop 46.1), the Schur multiplier `S_M(A) = T_{M·k_A}` on `S₂` (§3.1, Def 46.2), its `S_p` norm and unique bounded extension (§3.3, Def 46.3) and its completely bounded norm by amplified symbols (§1.4, §3.5), with duality, interpolation and the Grothendieck–Haagerup, Lafforgue–de la Salle, transference and Hörmander–Mikhlin–Schur theorems. No merged roadmap specifies Schur multipliers (NoncommutativeIntegration puts them outside its scope). Schatten classes `S_p`, `1 ≤ p < ∞`, are in Tau Ceti; the C*-algebra-level cb norm of CStarAlgebras C3.2 is in code.

## Mathlib
- Definition absent (Mathlib's only "Schur multiplier" is group-theoretic; `Matrix.hadamard` gives the finite entrywise product).
- Present: `MeasureTheory.Lp`, product measures, `L²` as a Hilbert space, compact operators `IsCompactOperator`, `ContinuousLinearMap` on Hilbert spaces, `Matrix.hadamard`, `Matrix.kroneckerMap`.
- Missing: Schatten classes, Hilbert–Schmidt operators, the kernel isomorphism.

## Prerequisite chain
1. `L²(Ω)`, product measure, `L^∞(Ω×Ω)`: in Mathlib.
2. Schatten classes `S_p`, `1 ≤ p < ∞`, Hilbert–Schmidt class, density of finite rank: in Tau Ceti (OperatorIdeals Parts B–C, implemented). Compact endpoint `S_∞`: PR #2421 §1.1.
3. Hilbert–Schmidt kernel isomorphism `L²(Ω×Ω,μ⊗μ) ≅ S_2(L²(Ω))` (Prop 46.1): PR #2421 §2.1, on AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.8 (open PR #925, σ-finite version requested as a gap); the trace `Tr` from VonNeumannAlgebras V4.Core (merged, not implemented).
4. Schur multiplier `S_M` on `S_2` (Def 46.2): PR #2421 §3.1.
5. `S_p → S_p` norm and extension by density (Def 46.3): PR #2421 §3.3 (needs §§1.1–1.2, 1.4, 2.2).
6. Completely bounded norm: PR #2421 §1.4, §3.5; the C*-level cb norm of CStarAlgebras C3.2 is in Tau Ceti.
7. Transference / Herz–Schur / Fourier multipliers on `Lᵖ(ℝⁿ)`: PR #2421 Layer 9; `L²` Fourier multipliers and Mihlin–Hörmander in Tau Ceti.

## Library-scale gap
PR #2421 is the roadmap for this definition and for the cited paper's main theorem (Pisier, *Similarity Problems and Completely Bounded Maps*, Ch. 5; Lafforgue–de la Salle, Duke 2011; Conde-Alonso–González-Pérez–Parcet–Tablate): what remains is its review and merging. Its kernel layer waits on AutomorphicSpectralTheory §0.8 (open PR #925) at σ-finite generality and on VonNeumannAlgebras V2/V4.Core; its interpolation layers on NoncommutativeIntegration Layers 1–2 (merged, not implemented).
