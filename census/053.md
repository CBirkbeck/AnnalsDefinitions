## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [Varifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/Varifolds/README.md) specifies the whole definition on a Riemannian manifold and on open subsets of Euclidean space: the Grassmann bundle (§§1.1–1.2), varifolds with weight, mass, support, restriction and varifold convergence (§§1.3–1.4, 53.1), rectifiable, integral and multiplicity-one varifolds `v(Γ, θ)` and `[Σ]` (§§2.2–2.3, 53.2), push-forward and dilations (§§3.1–3.2, 53.3), the first variation, stationarity and the generalized mean curvature (§§4.1, 4.3–4.4, 53.4), the derivative of mass along flows (§4.1, 53.5(a)), the first variation of a `C²` submanifold and stationarity iff minimality (§5.1, 53.5(b)), monotonicity and densities (§§6.1–6.3) and tangent cones of stationary integral varifolds (Layer 8, 53.6), regular and singular sets (§9.1), and the Simon and Edelen–Spolaor statements as acceptance tests (§9.4). Not implemented. Its first layers consume GeometricCurrents G6's Grassmann bundle (§1.2, §1.4) and GeometricMeasureTheory G2 with GeometricCurrents G2 (Layer 2), none of which is fully in code. Depends on #49, #52.

## Mathlib
No varifolds. Has Radon measures and weak convergence of finite measures, `MeasureTheory.Measure.hausdorffMeasure`, `Measure.map`, `Module.Grassmannian` (algebraic). No countably rectifiable sets or approximate tangent planes.

## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.CountablyRectifiable` (`Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/Basic.lean`).
- `TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian` with quotient topology (`Topology/Grassmannian/Basic.lean`), oriented/orthonormal variants.
- Euclidean currents: `mass` (`Analysis/Distribution/Mass/Basic.lean`), `FlatNormedCurrent` (`Analysis/Distribution/Flat/Completion.lean`), `formCurrent`.
- Approximate tangent planes: a.e. existence for countably rectifiable sets (`Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/ApproximateTangent.lean`) and a measurable choice of tangent projections (`Rectifiable/MeasurableTangent.lean`); Lipschitz area formula ℝ^m → ℝ^n with multiplicity (`MeasureTheory/Function/AreaFormula/`); coarea formula for Lipschitz maps with a.e. rectifiable fibres (`MeasureTheory/Function/Coarea/Lipschitz.lean`, `Geometry/MeasureTheory/Rectifiable/Coarea.lean`).
- Euclidean mass measure, normal currents and their weak compactness (`Analysis/Distribution/Mass/{Measure,Normal,Compactness}.lean`), smooth pushforward of compactly supported currents (`Analysis/Distribution/CutoffPushforward.lean`), the cone operator with mass bounds (`Mass/Cone.lean`), slicing of locally normal currents by a smooth function (`Mass/Slice.lean`).
- No varifold, Grassmann bundle over a manifold, or first variation.

## Merged roadmaps
- [Varifolds](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/Varifolds/README.md): Layer 1 (§1.1 the Grassmannian of a Euclidean space in projection form, compared with `TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian`; §1.2 the Grassmann bundle `G_k(M)` of a Riemannian manifold; §1.3 varifolds as Radon measures on `G_k(W)` with `weight`, mass, support and restriction; §1.4 varifold convergence `VarifoldTendsto` and compactness; 53.1), Layer 2 (§2.1 approximate tangent spaces of measures and the rectifiability criterion, §2.2 rectifiable and integral varifolds `v(Γ, θ)`, §2.3 the varifold `[Σ]` of a `C¹` submanifold; 53.2), Layer 3 (§3.1 push-forward with the `k`-Jacobian, §3.2 dilations and normal-coordinate blow-ups, §3.3 tangent spaces with multiplicity; 53.3), Layer 4 (§4.1 tangential divergence `div_S X` with the Levi-Civita connection, `firstVariation`, the derivative of mass along the flow of `X`; §4.3 stationarity, locally bounded first variation; §4.4 the Riesz decomposition with generalized mean curvature `H_V` and generalized boundary; §4.5 first variations for two metrics; 53.4, 53.5(a)), Layer 5 (§5.1 `δ[Σ] = −k ∫ ⟨H_Σ, X⟩`, stationary iff minimal; §5.2 varifolds on an embedded submanifold of Euclidean space; 53.5(b)), Layer 6 (Euclidean and Riemannian monotonicity, densities, constancy; 53.6), Layers 7–8 (Allard rectifiability and integral compactness, tangent cones of stationary integral varifolds; 53.6), §9.1 (`C^r`-regular and singular sets), §9.3 (comparison with GeometricCurrents G6), §9.4 (Simon's 2023 and Edelen–Spolaor's statements as acceptance tests). Not implemented. §1.1 needs only Mathlib and `TauCeti.finiteGrassmannian`; §§1.2 and 1.4 need GeometricCurrents G6 (Grassmann bundle, Radon compactness), and Layer 2 needs GeometricMeasureTheory G2 and GeometricCurrents G2.
- GeometricCurrents G6 "First variation, normal stability and interior regularity": Grassmann bundle compactness; auxiliary rectifiable varifold of an integral current (restriction, weight, integer multiplicity, weak measure convergence, pushforward); weak first variation under compactly supported flows, tangent-divergence formula, stationarity of minimizers, monotonicity, tangent cones. Not implemented. Explicitly "not a theorem about every stable varifold"; Varifolds §9.3 identifies its object.
- GeometricMeasureTheory G2: countably k-rectifiable sets, approximate tangent planes, area formula (Euclidean). Partially implemented: the density theorem, a.e. existence and a measurable choice of approximate tangent planes, the Lipschitz area formula and the Lipschitz coarea formula are in code; a.e. uniqueness of tangent planes, tangential differentiation and the tangential area formula are missing.
- GeometricMeasureTheory README states "Currents, varifolds ... are outside this roadmap".

## Prerequisite chain
Radon measures, Hausdorff measure [Mathlib] -> countably rectifiable sets [Tau Ceti `CountablyRectifiable`; GMT G2] -> approximate tangent planes, area formula [GMT G2, partial: existence and measurable choice of tangent planes and the Lipschitz area formula done; uniqueness and the tangential area formula missing] -> Grassmann bundle G_k(M) [Varifolds §§1.1–1.2, on GeometricCurrents G6, not implemented; fibre `finiteGrassmannian` in Tau Ceti] -> varifold = Radon measure on G_k, weight, mass, convergence, pushforward [Varifolds §§1.3–1.4, 3.1] -> rectifiable/integral varifold v(Γ,θ) [Varifolds §§2.1–2.3] -> first variation, stationarity, generalized mean curvature [Varifolds Layer 4, with #49's second fundamental form and #52's minimality in §5.1] -> monotonicity, tangent cones [Varifolds Layers 6–8].

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged Varifolds is the varifold roadmap (Allard theory: general varifolds, first variation as a vector-valued Radon measure, generalized mean curvature, monotonicity, Allard compactness for integral varifolds, tangent cones; Simon, *Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory*, Ch. 4 and 8; Allard 1972), consuming GeometricMeasureTheory G2 and GeometricCurrents G2 and G6. What remains is implementation. Allard regularity (its named Part II), stable varifolds (Schoen–Simon, Wickramasekera) and min-max theory are outside it and are what the Annals theorems use beyond the statements.
