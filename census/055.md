Not implemented. It proves the two-cell case of the Milman–Neeman theorem and leaves `3 ≤ q ≤ n+1` to a Part II. §2.1 and §3.1 need only Tau Ceti's Euclidean perimeter; §§2.2 and 3.2 consume De Giorgi's structure theorem from merged GeometricMeasureTheory G5, partly implemented.

## Merged roadmaps
- [GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters/README.md):
  - Layer 1: `I = φ ∘ Φ⁻¹`, Gaussian tails, orthogonal splitting.
  - Layer 2: §2.1 `weightedVariation W Ω u`, `weightedPerimeter W Ω U` by duality with `div_μ X = div X − ⟨∇W, X⟩`, `gaussianPerimeter` (55.1); §2.2 `weightedPerimeter_eq_lintegral_reducedBoundary` (`P_μ(U) = ∫_{∂*U} e^{−W} dμHE[n−1]`, 55.2); §§2.3–2.7 explicit perimeters, lower semicontinuity and compactness, weighted Gauss–Green, coarea, Gaussian invariance.
  - Layer 3: §3.1 `Cluster W q` (pairwise disjoint Lebesgue-measurable cells covering up to a null set, finite perimeter) and `Cluster.perimeter` (55.3); §3.2 interfaces `Σ_ij`, `A_ij = μ^{n−1}(Σ_ij)`, `P_μ(Ω) = Σ_{i<j} A_ij` (55.4); §3.3 interface quadratic form; §3.4 isoperimetric profiles; §3.5 existence of minimizing clusters.
  - Layer 4: §4.1 the canonical model cluster, §4.2 simplicial clusters in `E` (55.5), §§4.3–4.7 the measure map, model profile, its differential identities, and `I^{(q−1)} ≤ I_m^{(q−1)}`.
  - Layers 5–9: one-dimensional Gaussian isoperimetry, Ehrhard symmetrisation, the Gaussian isoperimetric theorem with equality and its two-cell cluster form (§§7.3–7.4), Borell–Sudakov–Tsirelson, Bobkov, concentration, Ehrhard's inequality.
  - Scope: the multi-bubble theorem for `3 ≤ q ≤ n + 1` (the paper's theorem) is "GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters, Part II", needing regularity of minimizing clusters (Almgren, Taylor, White, Colombo–Edelen–Spolaor) and the second-variation theory of Milman–Neeman §§5–10.
  - Not implemented (grep `weightedPerimeter`, `gaussianPerimeter`: none). Suppliers: GeometricMeasureTheory G5 (structure theorem, density one-half, essential versus reduced boundary), G2 (weighted coarea, tangential area formula), G6 (Lipschitz domains); Isoperimetry I4 (finite-perimeter slicing, essential convexity).
- GeometricMeasureTheory G5 (perimeter API, reduced boundary, De Giorgi structure theorem |Dχ_E| = H^{n−1}⌞∂*E): partial: the structure theorem itself (rectifiability of ∂*E and `|Dχ_E| = H^{n−1}⌞∂*E`) is not proved.
- Isoperimetry (sharp Euclidean isoperimetry): GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters consumes I4; no Gaussian or cluster content.

## Prerequisite chain
Lebesgue/Haar, stdGaussian [Mathlib; Gaussian density and projections in Tau Ceti] -> BV, perimeter [Tau Ceti `perimeter`; GMT G3/G5] -> reduced boundary, De Giorgi |Dχ| = H^{n−1}⌞∂*E [Tau Ceti `reducedBoundary`; GMT G5, structure theorem unproved] -> weighted perimeter P_γ = ∫ e^{−W} d|Dχ| (55.1/55.2) [GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters §§2.1–2.2] -> q-cluster, interfaces Σ_ij, total perimeter (55.3/55.4) [GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters §§3.1–3.2] -> isoperimetric profile, simplicial clusters (55.5) [GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters §3.4, §§4.1–4.2] -> the multi-bubble theorem for q ≥ 3 [unplanned; GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters' named Part II].

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged GaussianIsoperimetryAndClusters is the roadmap for it and for the two-cell case (Maggi, *Sets of Finite Perimeter and Geometric Variational Problems*, Part IV; Borell, Sudakov–Tsirelson; Ehrhard; Milman–Neeman). What remains is implementation, and De Giorgi's structure theorem in GeometricMeasureTheory G5. The paper's theorem for `3 ≤ q ≤ n + 1` needs its named Part II: regularity of minimizing clusters and the Milman–Neeman second-variation argument.
