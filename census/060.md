## Tau Ceti code
- `TauCeti.normalCauchyRiemannLinearization` (`Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Normal/Linearization.lean`): local-coordinate descent of the CR linearization to V ⧸ range(du x), shown independent of adding du·a; explicitly "global bundle gluing, Sobolev realizations and Fredholm theory are not asserted". Works pointwise even when u is not immersive, but no generalised normal bundle.
- The orthogonal normal bundle `ν_f` of a `C^n` immersion into a Riemannian manifold as a `C^m` vector bundle with its smooth projection (`TauCeti.orthogonalNormalContMDiffVectorBundle`, `Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Immersion/NormalBundle.lean`): the normal bundle of an immersed curve for a compatible metric, not defined at critical points.
- `cauchyRiemannLinearization` (`.../JHolomorphic/Linearization.lean`); normal rescaling of an almost complex structure along a pseudoholomorphic curve (`Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Normal/Rescaling.lean`).
- Inputs for the operator theory (see #56): smooth real CR operators, Sobolev sections on surfaces with and without boundary, the vector similarity principle for `C¹` solutions, and Fredholm determinant lines with their bundle and orientation transport along paths (`Analysis/Fredholm/DeterminantLine/{Bundle,Chart}.lean`).
- Riemann surfaces with boundary (PH2.4 carriers): the Schottky double of a Riemann surface with boundary as a Riemann surface without boundary, compact when the surface is, with the antiholomorphic sheet swap (`TauCeti.SchottkyDouble`, `SchottkyDouble.doubleChart`, `Geometry/Manifold/Complex/BorderedHolomorphic/SchottkyDouble.lean`), the input to PH7.1's doubled bundle pair; the closed unit disc as a Riemann surface with boundary (`Complex.UnitClosedDisc`, `.../BorderedHolomorphic/ClosedDisc.lean`).
- Formal adjoints: the formal adjoint of a real Cauchy–Riemann operator with Sobolev coefficients, after the reflection `(s, t) ↦ (−s, t)`, is again a real CR operator (`TauCeti.crFormalAdjoint_crReflectedAdjoint`, `Analysis/Complex/CauchyRiemann/RoughCoefficients/Adjoint.lean`), so cokernel elements are weak solutions of a reflected CR equation (local input to PH6.3).
- The Robbin–Salamon crossing-form formula for the Maslov index of a path of Lagrangian graphs, `Matrix.UnitaryGroup.maslovIndex_eq_crossingForm_add_finsum_of_souriau_eq_cayley` (`Analysis/Matrix/UnitaryGroup/Maslov/Crossing.lean`).
- Covariance of the local normal CR operator (`Geometry/Symplectic/JHolomorphic/Normal/Naturality.lean`): in the target, the map of normal quotients `normalFDerivMap` intertwines the normal linearizations at a pseudoholomorphic map; in the source, `normalCauchyRiemannLinearization_comp_right` and `normalCauchyRiemannLinearization_comp_right_of_surjective` identify the normal operator of `u ∘ ψ` with that of `u` under a source map intertwining the source structures, so local normal operators agree on overlaps of holomorphic source coordinates as well as target charts. At a critical point of `u` the source differential is not surjective onto a fixed quotient, which is where the generalised normal bundle is needed.
- Unique continuation for linear CR equations `∂ₛξ + J(z)∂ₜξ + C(z)ξ = 0` with a `C¹` field of complex structures `J` (the shape of the linearized operator of a J-curve): `TauCeti.eqOn_zero_of_contDiffOn_of_forall_isBigO_of_complexStructure`, `eqOn_zero_of_contDiffOn_of_eqOn_zero_of_complexStructure`, isolated zeros `setOf_ne_zero_mem_codiscreteWithin_of_contDiffOn_of_complexStructure` (`Analysis/Complex/CauchyRiemann/UniqueContinuation/VariableStructure.lean`), and its Floer specialization (`Geometry/Symplectic/Floer/UniqueContinuation.lean`); for `C¹` solutions only, and nothing for the J-curve itself (isolated critical points, HeegaardFloer F2.2a).
- Chart calculus of a smooth real CR operator on a bundle over a Riemann surface, `(D s)ᵢ = ∂̄ sᵢ + Zᵢ sᵢ` with `TauCeti.FiniteBundleAtlas.chartZeroOrder` smooth and bounded (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/CauchyRiemann/Chart.lean`); Rellich compactness `TauCeti.SobolevSection.isCompactOperator_lowerOrderL` for Sobolev sections over a compact boundaryless manifold (`Geometry/Manifold/VectorBundle/Sobolev/Rellich.lean`); weakly pseudoholomorphic `W^{1,p}_loc` maps `TauCeti.IsWeakPseudoholomorphicOn` (`Geometry/Manifold/Sobolev/Pseudoholomorphic.lean`).
- Model index computations: the cokernel of `∂̄` on the disc with a rotating-line boundary condition of Maslov index `k`, of real dimension `max (−1 − k) 0` (`TauCeti.exists_conjWirtingerDeriv_eq_and_eq_zpow_mul_conj_iff`, `Analysis/Complex/UnitDisc/Cokernel.lean`); the zero axiom of the Maslov index for paths `Matrix.UnitaryGroup.maslovIndex_eq_zero_of_rootMultiplicity_eq` (`Analysis/Matrix/UnitaryGroup/Maslov.lean`).

## Merged roadmaps
- TaubesGromov TG1 "Embedded normal operators": quotient normal bundle i*TX/TC of an embedding, normal real CR operator, Fredholm/adjoint realizations from PH6.2-PH6.3, normal index A_C² − κ·A_C. Embedded only. Not implemented.
- HeegaardFloer §4.6e (F2.6e): quotient real CR operator on the normal line of an embedded sphere, automatic surjectivity for degree ≥ −1. Embedded only.
- SymplecticFieldTheory 1.x: linearized operator at trivial cylinders on the normal bundle.
- HeegaardFloer §4.2 (F2.2a): isolated critical points and finite critical sets of a nonconstant `C¹` J-curve on a compact domain (MS Lemma 2.4.1), the input for 60.1. Not implemented.
- [AnalyticStableMaps](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AnalyticStableMaps/README.md) §0.2 (the linearized operator `D_u` of a closed curve of any genus as a real CR operator, with its closed index; #56), §0.4 (finite exceptional sets `Crit(u)` and `B` of a nonconstant closed curve, no immersion hypothesis), §0.5 (factorization through a simple curve, `IsSomewhereInjective`) and §2.23 (the normal operator at product slices `Σ × {p}`, an embedded example). Not implemented. No generalised normal bundle.
- Not specified: generalised normal bundle N_u ⊃ through critical points (60.1-60.2), independence of complement (60.3), normal operator for multiply covered/non-immersed curves (Wendl).

## Library-scale gap
A modest extension of TaubesGromov TG1 / HF F2.6e: construct T_u and N_u for nonconstant J-holomorphic curves with finitely many critical points and the normal operator with its index c₁(N_u) − Z(du) correction (Wendl, *Lectures on Holomorphic Curves*, §3.4; Ivashkovich-Shevchishin). Wendl's super-rigidity paper further needs the splitting of D_u^N on branched covers into representation-theoretic summands, which DGFloer's multiple-cover regularity item (Taubes-type) partially anticipates.
