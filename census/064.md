## Tau Ceti code
- The group Spin^c (64.1): `CliffordAlgebra.spinCGroup Q hQ` = (Spin(Q) × `Circle`)/`spinCKernel`, with `mk`, `ofSpin`, `ofCircle`, the determinant character `det` (`[s, z] ↦ z²`), `toSpecialOrthogonal`, and `mem_ker_toSpecialOrthogonal_prod_det_iff` (`LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/Spin/SpinC.lean`); its smooth compact Lie-group structure with the quotient map a local diffeomorphism and `contMDiff_realDet` (`Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/SpinC/Basic.lean`); half-spin actions descended to Spin^c (`Geometry/Lie/CliffordAlgebra/SpinC/HalfSpin.lean`).
- Spin structures on real vector bundles with fibre inner products, rank `n ≥ 1`: `TauCeti.SpinStructure` (a `TauCeti.PrincipalBundle` for `CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinGroupZero n` with a continuous map to `TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle` over the identity, equivariant along `CliffordAlgebra.spinVectorAction`), `SpinStructure.Equiv` with `refl`, `symm`, `trans`, `toHomeomorph`, and `SpinStructure.pullback`, `Equiv.pullback` (`Topology/VectorBundle/SpinStructure.lean`). This is SpinGeometry G4.2's structure; replacing `Spin(n)` by `Spin^c(n)` and the frame map's equivariance by `toSpecialOrthogonal` gives 64.2.
- The canonical `Spinᶜ` lift of the unitary group of an almost Hermitian four-space: `TauCeti.SeibergWitten.unitaryLift : U(V, J) → Spinᶜ(4)`, `canonicalLineFixingSubgroup`, `canonicalLineFixingSubgroupEquiv`, and `det_unitaryLift` (the determinant character of the lift is the complex determinant) (`Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/UnitaryLift.lean`): the structure-group level of the canonical Spin^c structure of an almost complex 4-manifold.
- No Spin^c structure (principal lift), no H² torsor, no Turaev model (grep `SpinCStructure`, Turaev vector-field model: none).
- Seiberg-Witten algebra on a fixed four-dimensional Spin^c spinor module (`Analysis/PDE/SeibergWitten/ChargeConjugation.lean`, `Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Trace/Free.lean`) without the global structure.
- Spin side: `TauCeti.CliffordAlgebra.realCliffordSpinContinuousMulEquivUnitsRange` (cited by SpinGeometry G4); the orthonormal frame bundle as a principal `O(n)`/`U(n)`-bundle in `TauCeti.PrincipalBundle` form (`TauCeti.OrthonormalFrameBundle.Fiber`, `Topology/VectorBundle/OrthonormalFrame/Principal.lean`), with proper projection.
