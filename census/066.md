## Mathlib
Has manifolds with boundary (`ModelWithCorners`, `modelWithCornersEuclideanHalfSpace`, `ModelWithCorners.boundary`/`interior` API), Riemannian metrics, Levi-Civita (defined at boundary points). No boundary second fundamental form or boundary normal coordinates.

## Tau Ceti code
- Flat-ambient second fundamental form and shape operator only (`TauCeti.secondFundamentalForm`/`shapeOperator`, and on manifold sources `TauCeti.manifoldSecondFundamentalForm`/`manifoldShapeOperator` in `Geometry/Manifold/Immersion/`), plus smooth normal projections along immersions into a Riemannian manifold (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Immersion/Projection.lean`).
- Suppliers RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary pins: the Riemannian Hessian `TauCeti.Manifold.hessian` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Laplacian.lean`) with its conformal change `Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.hessian_rescale` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Hessian/Conformal.lean`), the induced boundary metric `Bundle.ContMDiffRiemannianMetric.restrictBoundaryTangentSpace` (`Geometry/Manifold/Riemannian/Boundary.lean`), `TauCeti.IsOutwardPointing`, regular sublevel sets as manifolds with boundary `TauCeti.isManifold_scalarRegularSublevel` (`Geometry/Manifold/RegularValue/Scalar/`), and `TauCeti.EuclideanHalfSpace.collarDiffeomorph` (`Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Collar/Basic.lean`).
- Collars: the structure `TauCeti.SmoothCollar` (a `C^k` injective local diffeomorphism `∂M × [0, 1) → M` restricting to the boundary inclusion) with `isOpenEmbedding`, `opensRange`, `diffeomorphOpensRange`, `reparam`, `isCollar` and `ofIsEmpty` (`Geometry/Manifold/Boundary/Collar/Smooth.lean`); local smooth product collars `TauCeti.IsProductCollarChart.diffeomorphProd` (`Collar/Diffeomorph.lean`); topological collars (`IsCollar`/`IsCollared`, Brown's collaring theorem, `boundaryCollar`). There is no existence theorem for a global smooth collar (the module docstring of `Collar/Diffeomorph.lean` says the global theorem "still requires patching these local diffeomorphisms") and no uniqueness up to isotopy.
- No II_{∂M}, boundary defining function, strict convexity or boundary normal coordinates (grep `IsStrictlyConvexAt`, `boundaryDefiningFunction`: none).

## Merged roadmaps
- [RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary/README.md): Layer 1 (§1.1 tangent/inward/outward directions, §1.2 unit normals, §1.3 boundary defining functions with existence and comparison `ρ' = uρ`, §1.4 `secondFundamentalForm` `II_q(u,w) = −Hess ρ(u,w)/|dρ|` with the equivalent descriptions `⟨∇_u N, w⟩` for an extension `N` of `ν_out` and `⟨∇_u W, ν_in⟩`, symmetry, smoothness, naturality, unit ball `II = h`; §1.5 Weingarten map, principal and mean curvature; §1.6 conformal change), Layer 2 (§2.1 `IsStrictlyConvexAt`, convex, strictly concave, `IsStrictlyConvexBoundary`; §2.2 `isStrictlyConvexAt_iff_hessian_neg` for one or every defining function; §2.3 openness, uniform bound `II ≥ c h` on compact boundaries, families of metrics), Layer 3 (regular domains, collar extension and double, realisation as `{ρ̃ ≥ 0}` in a boundaryless manifold, §3.6 comparison with R5a), Layer 4 (§4.2 `(ρ∘γ)''(0) = −|dρ| II(u,u)`, tangent geodesics leave, no interior tangency; §4.3 signed distance; §4.4 boundary normal coordinates `g = dr² + h_r`, `II = −½ ∂_r h_r|₀`; §4.5 convexity functions with strictly concave level sets; §4.6 slight enlargements), Layer 5 (exit time, non-trapping, scattering relation and lens data), Layer 6 (balls, caps, hyperbolic domains, small geodesic balls, conformal metrics). This is 66.1–66.3 and the Remarks, and makes the hypotheses of Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy Theorems 1.1–1.3 expressible (Downstream consumers name future X-ray transform and boundary rigidity roadmaps). Not implemented (grep `IsStrictlyConvexAt`, `boundaryDefiningFunction`: none). Suppliers: DifferentialGeometry 5.1, 12.2–12.3, 12.5; RiemannianGeometry R5a, R5c, R7.9; GeometricTopology 1.1, 1.3.
- RiemannianGeometry R5a (B, S of immersions) and R5a.2 (principal curvatures), in RiemannianGeometry's boundaryless standing setting: applied to `∂M` inside an extension `M̃` they give 66.1 up to sign (R5a has `B(X,Y)` = normal part of `∇_X Y`; def #66 uses the outward-normal Weingarten convention). Not implemented. RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §3.6 compares its `II` with R5a.
- RiemannianGeometry R5c (items 1 and 3) presents a compact Riemannian manifold with boundary as the closure of a relatively compact smooth domain of a boundaryless manifold, by a collar and K5a's Seeley extension; R7.9 gives normal collars and defining functions of two-sided hypersurfaces. Suppliers of RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary Layers 3–4. Not implemented.
- GeometricTopology Layer 1.1 smooth collars and 1.3 gluing along the boundary (collar extension, double), needed for 66.2(b)–(c) through RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §§3.3–3.5. Layer 1.1 is partial: the `SmoothCollar` structure is in code, its existence is not.
- GeometricCurrents G7: strict mean convexity "⟨tr B_{∂K}, ν⟩ < 0" of a compact domain as a barrier hypothesis. Mean-convex only, inside a boundaryless ambient.
- [RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/RiemannianGeometry/MinimalSurfaces/README.md) MS10.1 (Reilly's formula) consumes the second fundamental form of the boundary with `A(X,Y) = ⟨D_Xν,Y⟩` for the outward normal (def #66's sign). A consumer: no convexity predicate.

## Prerequisite chain
manifold with boundary, Riemannian metric, Hessian, induced boundary metric [Mathlib, Tau Ceti] -> smooth collar existence, collar extension and double [GT Layers 1.1 and 1.3, merged; `SmoothCollar` structure in Tau Ceti, existence not] -> outward unit normal [DifferentialGeometry 12.5, not in code] -> boundary defining functions, II_{∂M}, Weingarten map (66.1, 66.2(a)) [RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary §§1.2–1.5] -> strictly convex / convex / strictly concave boundary, Hessian characterisation (66.3) [§§2.1–2.3] -> realisation in a boundaryless extension [Layer 3, on R5c and R7.9] -> geodesics tangent to ∂M leave (66.2(c)), boundary normal coordinates (66.2(b)) [§4.2, §4.4].

## Library-scale gap
None for the definition: merged RiemannianManifoldsWithConvexBoundary is the roadmap for this theory: boundary geometry of Riemannian manifolds with boundary, convexity of the boundary and its consequences for geodesics, boundary normal coordinates, the exit time and the scattering relation (Paternain–Salo–Uhlmann, *Geometric Inverse Problems*, Ch. 3; Lee, *Introduction to Riemannian Manifolds*, Ch. 8; Uhlmann–Vasy). What remains is implementation. The paper's theorems also need a geodesic X-ray transform and boundary rigidity roadmap (Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy), which it names as a future consumer.
