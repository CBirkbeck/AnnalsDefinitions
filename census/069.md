## Status
ROADMAP-PR
The λ-adic representation attached to an eigenform away from the level (weight ≥ 2, nebentypus, arithmetic-Frobenius polynomial `X² − λ_p X + ε(p)p^{k−1}`) is the milestone `higherWeight_attachment` of open PR #928 §6.2, re-exported in PR #1007 §1.7. No merged roadmap constructs it: merged ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations supplies the continuous-representation carrier, lattices and Čebotarev uniqueness, and merged IntegralHeckeAndGaloisDeterminants only matches the rank-two Frobenius-polynomial convention against a representation supplied by a "classical geometric owner". Tau Ceti has the eigenform/newform side and the `ContinuousRep` carrier, but no Galois representation attached to a form.
