## Status
ROADMAP-PR
Abelian schemes over an `O_{L^Gal}[1/d_L]`-algebra `R ⊂ ℂ` with injective `O_L`-action and Lie algebra of lifted CM type `Σ` (Kings–Sprang Def. 1.8, Prop. 1.11) are specified in open PR #1254 §1.2 and used as the "standard datum" of PR #1543 §0.2. Merged roadmaps cover the case `L` a CM field: CM types of CM algebras (70.1(a)) are target §5.1 `CMType` of merged ShimuraVarieties, and `O_F`-abelian schemes over a general base whose Lie algebra has a given signature type, for rank one the CM type, are target M1.3 of merged PELModuli. Lifted CM types of a non-CM `L` (70.1(b)) and 70.3 for such `L` are only in the PRs. Abelian schemes over a general base with their Lie algebra, dual and `H¹_dR` are targets of merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli Layers A0–A4. Tau Ceti has abelian varieties over fields only.

Not implemented.
- [PELModuli](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/PELModuli/README.md) (merged PR #966) M1.3 "Unitary `O_F`-abelian schemes and signature type" (README l.1638): `OFAbelianScheme` (`(A, i)` with `i : O_F → End(A)` unital) over any scheme over a subring of `ℚ`, for a CM field `F`, and `HasSignatureType Ψ`, the determinant condition on `Lie_{A/S}` (Remark 2's Kottwitz form); for a rank-one generalized CM type `Ψ` (M0.25, l.1422, the indicator of a ShimuraVarieties §5.1 CM type) this is Def. 70.3(b) for `L = F` CM, with `dim A = [F⁺ : ℚ]` (`HasSignatureType.dim`) and the `τ`-part decomposition at unramified `p` (M0.27, l.1481). It does not treat non-CM `L`. Not implemented.
- [AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli/README.md) supplies the carrier: §A0.1 `AbelianScheme S` over an arbitrary base (README l.600), §A1.3 invariant differentials with `Lie(A/S) = ω_A^∨` (l.789), §A1.3a Serre tensor for ring actions (l.804), §A2.2 the dual abelian scheme (l.1093), §A4.3 `H¹_dR(A/S)` with `0 → ω_A → H¹_dR → Lie(A^∨) → 0` (l.1916), §A4.7a realizations of the Serre tensor (l.2291), §A6.37 the CM isotypic decomposition over `ℂ` (l.3849). None is implemented.
- Other prerequisites: JacobianChallenge (abelian varieties over a field; nothing representable); SchemeAndStackFoundations (descent, representability); Completed/GlobalNumberFields (number-field arithmetic, differents).

## Open roadmap PRs
