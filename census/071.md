## Status
ROADMAP-PR
`A_f⁰ := J₀(N)/B_f⁰` for a weight-2 newform with trivial character (the quotient by the abelian subvariety generated by `t(J₀(N))`, `t ∈ ker θ_f`), the definition as stated, is specified only in open PR #913 Layer 16 (ModularCurvesPartII; stated for primitive level `N ≥ 5`). Merged CyclotomicClassGroupsAndStickelberger §4.3 specifies the `J₁(N)` version (Remark 4 of the definition): the Albanese quotient `A_f` of the Jacobian of `X₁(N)` by the Hecke-ideal images of the eigencharacter kernel, for every weight-two newform with character, with `dim A_f = [E:ℚ]` and the general abelian subvariety quotient `B/C`. For trivial character it is isogenous to the paper's `A_f`, and the paper uses `A_f` only up to isogeny. Flag: borderline; filed as ROADMAP-PR because the `J₀(N)` quotient and the Hecke action on `J₀(N)` are only in PR #913. Nothing in code.
