## Status
ROADMAP-PR
Emerton's completed cohomology (ϖ-adic completion of the p-level colimit of cohomology of modular curves at tame level `K^p`, with `GL₂(ℚ_p)`, `G_ℚ` and Hecke actions) is specified in open PR #1035 (CompletedCohomologyPartII) §2.1 (general locally symmetric towers) and PR #1270 Layer 1 (modular curves over `ℚ`, Emerton's normalizations). Nothing in code. No merged roadmap specifies it: merged ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces builds the finite-level spaces `X_K` and their Betti cohomology but lists "Level systems, completed cohomology, derived limits over levels" as not treated, and merged ModularCurvesPartII builds the level-`H` modular curves for every open `H ⊆ GL₂(Ẑ)` with finite and adic étale cohomology but no colimit over levels.
