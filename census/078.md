Definitions 78.2–78.4 (closure as `Scheme.Hom.image` in the abelian-scheme model over `O_{K,S}`, its flatness and uniqueness, good reduction via `Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus` / `AlgebraicGeometry.Smooth`, and the equivalent forms) are stated almost verbatim in open PR #852 (Néron models and semistable abelian varieties); open PR #2461 §10.1 defines good reduction outside `S` of an ample smooth hypersurface the same way. The scheme-theoretic tools are in Mathlib; abelian schemes over `O_{K,S}` and the uniqueness of their extension from the generic fibre are targets of merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli (§§A0.1, A1.8), not implemented.

## Open roadmap PRs
- #852 "Néron models and semistable abelian varieties": target (≈ line 1035) defines the closure of `Z ⊆ A` in the model `𝒜` over `S = Spec O_{K,S}` via `Scheme.Hom.image`, proves flatness, uniqueness, flat base change; defines good reduction of `H` at `s` via `Scheme.Hom.smoothLocus` and over `S` via `AlgebraicGeometry.Smooth`; proves the equivalent forms, smooth proper relative effective Cartier divisor of relative dimension `dim A − 1`, and independence of `S`. Needs 1.4, 1.8 (abelian scheme extension = Néron model, uniqueness), 1.9.
- #2461 "MordellLawrenceVenkateshPartII" §10.1 "Good reduction and primitive hypersurfaces": for `A/K` with abelian scheme model `𝒜` over `O_K[S⁻¹]` and a smooth ample effective divisor `H ⊂ A`, `H` has good reduction outside `S` when its schematic closure in `𝒜` is smooth (Lawrence–Sawin Def. 9.1), with translation stabilizers and primitivity, consuming AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A2.4. This is Definition 78.3 over `S` (ample `H`), without the pointwise form at a single place.
- #1809 "Sheaf convolution on abelian varieties" (cites Lawrence–Sawin; consumer of the setting).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Scheme-theoretic image, smooth locus, flatness: in Mathlib.
2. Effective Cartier divisors: in Tau Ceti.
3. Abelian varieties over `K`: in Tau Ceti; abelian schemes over `O_{K,S}`: target in merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A0.1 (not implemented).
4. Good-reduction model = Néron model, uniqueness: target in PR #852 §§1.4, 1.8; uniqueness of an abelian-scheme model also follows from merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A1.8.
5. Closure and good reduction of hypersurfaces: target in PR #852; good reduction outside `S` of ample hypersurfaces also in PR #2461 §10.1.
6. `NS(A_{K̄})` and "represents φ" (used by the paper, not by the definition): merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli §A2.10 `AbelianNS` (with the Mumford map `AbelianNS.toSymmetricHom`) and §A6.16 finiteness, for abelian varieties of any dimension (not implemented).
