# 83. Schwartz (Hecke) algebra of an adelic reductive group

## Status
ROADMAP-MERGED. Merged [AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups/README.md) §0.3 specifies `AdelicSchwartzFunction(G)`, the rapidly decreasing group Schwartz algebra of `G(𝔸_F)` with fixed compact finite support and bi-level, its convolution, involution and density of test functions; §0.2 gives `C_c^∞(G(𝔸^∞))` as a restricted tensor product of local Hecke algebras. Its seminorms are height-weighted (`sup H(g)^r |L(u)R(v)f(g)|`) rather than Def. 83.2's bounded algebraic differential operators, and §0.3 flags the completed-tensor identification as a separate comparison: open PR #1761 "AutomorphicSpectralTheoryPartII" §1.1 identifies the archimedean factor with Def. 83.2 (with the Sobolev description of Remark (2)), §1.9 gives the adelic LF comparison with Def. 83.4, and §4.3 the multipliers of [2021, 194(2), no. 5]. Borderline: the merged target is the definition up to these comparisons. Nothing is implemented.

5. `S(G_∞)` via algebraic differential operators (83.1–83.2): target in PR #1761 §1.1 (as a description of the real factor of AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §0.3).
6. Adelic `S(G(𝔸_F))`, level-`K` part, actions `R(f)` and `π(f)` (83.4–83.5): target in merged AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §0.3 (not started), with the comparisons in PR #1761 §§1.2, 1.9.

## Library-scale gap
None for the space itself, which is merged AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §0.3. One reconciliation is in open PR #1761 only: Def. 83.4 is the *algebraic* tensor product `S(G_∞) ⊗ C_c^∞(G(𝔸^∞))` with `S(G_∞)` defined by algebraic differential operators (83.2), while §0.3 defines an inductive union of height-weighted fixed-support, fixed-level stages. #1761 §1.1 identifies the real factor and §1.9 identifies each stage with finitely many copies of `S(G_∞)`, which gives the same space; #1761 consumes §0.3 as its carrier.
