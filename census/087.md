# 87. Tate–Shafarevich group of an abelian variety over a number field

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Tau Ceti already has `Sha(E/K)` for an elliptic curve over a number field, with all places, local terms over `K̄_v`, embedding independence and `Sha[m]` finite. Mathlib has the Galois-module Tate–Shafarevich group (the different construction of Remark 2). The abelian-variety version, with `φ`-Selmer groups and finiteness of `Sel^φ` and `Sha[n]` (87.2–87.4), is §2.12a "Abelian Kummer and Sha interfaces" of open PR #869 "Selmer groups, Selmer complexes and Iwasawa cohomology". The merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §6.4b names `Ш(A/F)` of an abelian variety over a number field as the all-place localization kernel of `H¹(F,A)`, identified with Mathlib's `ContinuousCohomology.tateShafarevich` through the comparison with completions, and compares it with the Néron-model kernel `L_S(A)`; it has no `φ`-Selmer groups and no finiteness statements (87.2–87.4), so no merged roadmap specifies all of the definition. Nothing beyond elliptic curves is implemented.

## Mathlib
- `ContinuousCohomology.tateShafarevich` (`RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/Sha.lean`), with `mem_tateShafarevich` and `tateShafarevich_eq_ker_pi`. This is the kernel of `H^n(G, A) → ∏ H^n(G_v, A)` for one module `A`, the Galois-module version that the writer's note warns about.
- `IsDedekindDomain.selmerGroup` `K(S,n)` (`RingTheory/DedekindDomain/SelmerGroup.lean`).
- Missing: abelian varieties and their Galois cohomology.

## Tau Ceti code
- Elliptic: `WeierstrassCurve.sha` (`AlgebraicGeometry/EllipticCurve/GaloisCohomology/Selmer/NumberField.lean`), the kernel of `H¹(G_K, E(Kˢ))` to the local `H¹` over completions at all places with fixed embeddings of separable closures. Also `sha_eq_shafarevichTateGroup` (independence of embeddings), `finite_torsionBy_sha`, the `m`-Selmer group `selmerGroup` and `kummerSelmerMap` (`Selmer/Basic.lean`), and for an isogeny `φ` the groups `selmerGroup`, `selmerShaMap` to `shafarevichTateGroupKer` (`Selmer/Isogeny/Basic.lean`), i.e. 87.4(i) for elliptic isogenies, with `finite_shafarevichTateGroupKer` and `card_selmerGroup` (`Selmer/Isogeny/Finite.lean`). Functoriality along morphisms: `TauCeti.Isogeny.Hom.shafarevichTateMap`, `finite_shafarevichTateGroup_iff` (87.4(iv) for elliptic curves) and `exists_isogeny_shafarevichTateMap_eq_zero` (`GaloisCohomology/Isogeny/Sha.lean`), used for the isogeny behaviour of the BSD quotient (`EllipticCurve/BirchSwinnertonDyer/Isogeny.lean`). General Selmer structures: `TauCeti.ContCohomology` `selmerGroup` (`RepresentationTheory/Homological/ContCohomology/SelmerStructure.lean`).
- Abelian varieties: `TauCeti.AlgebraicGeometry.AbelianVariety`, isogenies (`AbelianVariety/Isogeny.lean`). No Galois cohomology of `A(K̄)`, no `Sha(A/K)`.

Not implemented; needs its own §1.9 and §§2.1–2.2 (Néron models, component groups), SchemeAndStackFoundations §§0.12–0.14, 1.15–1.16, 2.1–2.4, 2.10, 2.15 and ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.3.7 (Lang's theorem).

## Prerequisite chain
1. Continuous cohomology of profinite groups with discrete coefficients, Galois-module Sha: Mathlib (+ Tau Ceti `ContCohomology`).
2. Places, completions, decomposition groups and embedding independence: Tau Ceti (GlobalNumberFields; elliptic Sha code).
3. Abelian varieties, isogenies: Tau Ceti (basic). `A(L)` as a functor with group structure: target PR #869 §2.12a (`AbelianVariety.Points`).
4. Elliptic `Sha(E/K)`, `Sel_m`, `Sel_φ`: Tau Ceti (EllipticCurves Layer 7, implemented).
5. `H¹(K,A)`, `res_v`, `Sha(A/K)`, `Sel^φ(A/K)`, 87.4(i)–(iii): target PR #869 §2.12a; `Ш(A/F)` and its identification with the Galois-module version also merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §6.4b. The local comparison needs smooth approximation and Hensel lifting for torsors, also in §2.12a.
6. Torsor interpretation of `H¹(K,A)` (87.1, second sentence): target PR #1017 Layer 3 (torsor descent); not needed for the definition.
7. 87.4(iv) isogeny invariance of finiteness: unplanned as a named target, a corollary of 5.
