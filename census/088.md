# 88. Weak functorial transfer to GL_N

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #1756 "AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactorsPartII: roadmap", §10.1 "weak functorial transfer", is CFK Definition 2.2. For split `Sp_{2n}`, `SO_{2n}`, `SO_{2n+1}`, `GSpin_{2n}`, `GSpin_{2n+1}` and the standard `r_G`, a weak transfer is automorphic `Π` on `GL_N` with matching Satake classes at almost all unramified places and the `r_G`-image infinitesimal character at every archimedean place. This is exactly the generality of the cited paper. The general notion for an arbitrary L-representation `r : ᴸG → GL_N` (88.1 in general) is only partly specified: merged AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactors AL.4.1.1 defines the unramified Euler factor `det(1 − T r(t))` of a Satake class `t` for an arbitrary finite-dimensional `r` of the unramified L-group, but not the transferred local representation `r_*(π_v)`, and PR #1180 §1.6 defines weak lifts along algebraic `R : GL_n → GL_N`. The L-group itself is a merged target (ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.5), not implemented. Nothing is implemented.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Lie algebras, enveloping algebras, `GL_N`: Mathlib.
2. Split reductive groups, root data, pinnings: Tau Ceti (ReductiveGroups, largely done).
3. Dual group and L-group: target ReductiveGroupsPartII RG2.5 (merged, not implemented).
4. Harish-Chandra isomorphism and infinitesimal characters (Def. #5): LieHighestWeight Layer 7 (merged, done for semisimple Lie algebras), §9.2 reductive centres (merged, not implemented) and AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §2.2 (merged, not implemented).
5. Adelic points: merged AdelicAlgebraicGroups AA.1 (not started). Automorphic representations with local components (Def. #67): merged AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups §§3.2, 3.4–3.5 and ArithmeticLocallySymmetricSpaces ALS.F.6 (not started).
6. Unramified representations, Satake parameters, unramified transfer `r_*(π_v)` (88.1): Satake in merged SmoothRepresentationsOfLocalGroups SR.4.1–SR.4.2 (not started; needs RG2.5); the Euler factor `det(1 − T r(t))` for a general `r` in merged AutomorphicLFunctionsAndLocalFactors AL.4.1.1 (not started). The transfer along `r` is implicit in PR #1756 §10.1 for standard `r_G`, and in PR #1180 §1.6 for algebraic `R` on `GL_n`.
7. Archimedean matching of infinitesimal characters (88.2): PR #1756 §0.4 (standard `r_G`) asserts that `T(π)` has the `r_G`-image infinitesimal character but does not specify the map `𝔥^* → 𝔱̂` on infinitesimal characters; the general map is unplanned.
8. Weak functorial transfer (88.3): target PR #1756 §10.1 (split classical groups, standard `r_G`), which depends on open PRs #999 and #1063; the general L-representation version is unplanned.

## Library-scale gap
Small, if generality matters: a general "transfer along an L-representation `r : ᴸG → GL_N`" (unramified transfer through Satake parameters `t_v ⋊ Frob_v`, and the `dr` image of infinitesimal characters through `𝔥^* = 𝔱̂`) would naturally sit in #1756 Layer 0 or in AutomorphicFormsOnReductiveGroups next to infinitesimal characters (§2.2), specialising to §10.1. Reference: Borel, *Automorphic L-functions* (Corvallis), §§2–7.
