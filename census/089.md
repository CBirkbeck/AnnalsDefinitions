# 89. ℓ-adic Galois representation with values in the Mumford–Tate group

## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #933 "Roadmap: complex Shimura varieties and canonical models", §7.0i.1 "Mumford–Tate-valued ℓ-adic Galois representations and Frobenius classes", states Def. 89.2–89.4 essentially verbatim. That covers `E^𝐆`, `ρ_ℓ^𝐆`, `Conj_𝐆 = 𝐆//𝐆` with `χ_𝐆`, and `γ_ℓ(v)`, with Checks matching Remarks 2–3. §7.0i covers Deligne's absolute Hodge theorem (89.1). The conjugacy quotient `Conj_H = H//H` with `χ_H` (89.3) is a merged target (ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §1.11), as are the Tate module, Frobenius semisimplicity and complex uniformization of abelian varieties (AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli) and the Artin comparison (ComplexComparisonPartII §C6.6). The Mumford–Tate group (Def. #13) is PR #1038 §1.2 / #933 §P.3a. Nothing is implemented.

## Mathlib
- Has: absolute Galois groups, continuous representations into `GL_n(ℚ_ℓ)` (as continuous homomorphisms), `ConjClasses`, invariants of group actions on rings.
- Missing: étale cohomology, Betti cohomology of complex varieties with Hodge structure, Mumford–Tate groups, categorical/GIT quotients `Spec O(G)^G`.

## Tau Ceti code
- Hodge structures (`Geometry/Hodge/Structure.lean`, from Completed/HodgeStructures), abelian varieties (`AlgebraicGeometry/AbelianVariety/`), affine group schemes as Hopf algebras and Hopf ideals (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/HopfIdeal/`), `GeneralLinear.coordinateHopfAlgebra`.
- Conjugation coaction `TauCeti.HopfAlgebra.conjugationAlgHom` (`Algebra/AlgebraicGroup/Hopf/Conjugation.lean`) and finite generation of coinvariants for linearly reductive Hopf algebras (`TauCeti.ComoduleAlgebra.finiteType_coinvariants`, `Algebra/Bialgebra/ComoduleAlgebra/LinearlyReductive.lean`): the inputs of `Conj_G`.
- Missing: Mumford–Tate group (searched `MumfordTate`), `H¹_ét(A_Ē, ℚ_ℓ)` or `V_ℓA` as a Galois representation, the Betti–étale comparison, `Conj_G` itself.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Galois groups, continuous homomorphisms: Mathlib.
2. Rational Hodge structures, abelian varieties, Hopf-algebra group schemes: Tau Ceti.
3. Mumford–Tate group (Def. #13): target PR #1038 §1.2 and PR #933 §P.3a.
4. `H¹_B(A(ℂ),ℚ)` with Hodge structure, `H¹_ét(A_Ē,ℚ_ℓ)` (or `V_ℓA^∨`), comparison isomorphism: targets in merged AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli (Layer A5, A6.11) and ComplexComparisonPartII §C6.6, as cited by #933 (not implemented).
5. Deligne: Hodge cycles on abelian varieties are absolutely Hodge (89.1): target PR #933 §7.0i.
6. Conjugation quotient `Conj_G`, `χ_G`: target in merged ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §1.11 (not implemented; conjugation coaction and invariant finite generation in Tau Ceti) and PR #2131 Layer 0.
7. Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich, Frobenius at good-reduction places: merged NeronModelsAndSemistableAbelianVarieties §5.1; merged ArithmeticGaloisRepresentations §§2.1–2.2 and AbelianSchemesAndArithmeticModuli A6.21.
8. `ρ_ℓ^𝐆`, `γ_ℓ(v)`: target PR #933 §7.0i.1.

## Library-scale gap
None beyond the PRs. The chain is very long: Deligne's absolute Hodge theorem needs Betti/de Rham/étale comparisons for abelian varieties and the CM/Weil-class reduction.
