## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2418 (CountableStructureSpacesPartII) specifies the definition: §1.2 `BorelComplete K` for `K : Set (RelationalConfiguration L ℕ)` (the isomorphism relation `classIso K'` of every invariant Borel class of every countable relational language Borel reduces to `classIso K`), exactly Definition 94.2 over Tau Ceti's `Setoid.BorelReducible` (Def 94.1, in code), with transfer, bireducibility and language-renaming API; §1.3 its extension to languages with function symbols through a functional coding space and the graph-code map (needed for the group language of torsion-free abelian groups); Layers 2–3 the Friedman–Stanley theorem (graphs, trees and linear orders are Borel complete); Layer 4 the Becker–Kechris universal embedding; Layer 5 the equivalent characterisations (94.4); Layer 6 consequences (not Borel, above E₀, continuum many classes, unbounded Scott height). No merged roadmap specifies it.
