## Status
ROADMAP-PR. Open PR #2431 (DynamicalDegreesOfRationalMaps) specifies the definition: the graph `Γ_f ⊆ X ×_K X` of a dominant rational map with its two projections (§1.2, 95.1), Euler-characteristic intersection numbers with real coefficients (Layer 2), the positivity package (Layer 3), the mixed degrees `deg_k(f; L, M)` and `deg_{k,ω}(f)` (`mixedDegree`, §4.1, 95.2) with their independence of the model, sub-multiplicativity (§4.4, 95.3), and `dynamicalDegree K d k f ω = lim deg_{k,ω}(f^n)^{1/n}` with existence, independence of `ω`, log-concavity and birational invariance (Layer 5, 95.4). It takes `N¹(X/K)`, nefness and Kleiman's nonnegativity from the merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII §§1.1, 1.5, 1.7 (not implemented) and degrees of first-Chern intersections from SchemeAndStackFoundations §§5.2–5.3. No merged roadmap names dynamical degrees.

## Prerequisite chain
1. Normal projective variety over K, dominant rational self-map, composition/iterates: Mathlib (`Scheme.RationalMap`, `Birational/Composition`, `IsDominant`).
2. Graph Γ_f as integral closed subscheme of X×X, birational π_1, generically finite π_2 (95.1): PR #2431 §§1.2–1.3.
3. Cartier divisors and line bundle classes: Tau Ceti (`Scheme.CartierDivisor`, `LineBundleClass`).
4. Top intersection numbers `(D_1⋯D_d)`: degrees of first-Chern intersections, SchemeAndStackFoundations §§5.2–5.3 (merged; §5.2 partial, §5.3 not started); Euler-characteristic form and real coefficients: PR #2431 Layer 2.
5. `N^1(X)_ℝ`, nef, ample and big classes, Kleiman: AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII §§1.1, 1.5–1.7 (merged, not implemented); real nef classes and approximation: PR #2431 §§3.1–3.2.
6. Pull-back along the projections of Γ_f, birational invariance: PR #2431 §§4.1–4.3.
7. `deg_{k,ω}(f)` (95.2), sub-multiplicativity (95.3), `λ_k(f)` (95.4): PR #2431 Layers 4–5.

## Library-scale gap
Covered by PR #2431 (Lazarsfeld, *Positivity in Algebraic Geometry I* ch. 1-2; Fulton, *Intersection Theory*; Dinh–Sibony, Truong and Dang for sub-multiplicativity), on top of the merged AlgebraicModuliForArithmeticGeometryPartII Layer 1 and SchemeAndStackFoundations Layer 5: what remains for the definition is review and merging of PR #2431, and implementation of those merged sections. The paper's spectral interpretation (Banach spaces of b-divisor classes, Dang–Favre) is beyond PR #2431.
