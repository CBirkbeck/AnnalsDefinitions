## Status
PARTIAL-CODE. For countable relational languages, `TauCeti.RelationalConfiguration L ℕ` is Mod_L(ω) with the product topology, Polish and standard Borel instances, the relabelling action of `Equiv.Perm ℕ` (Polish S∞), `isoSetoid = orbitRel`, `IsomorphismInvariant` and Borel `modelsOf φ`. Missing: languages with function symbols (the cited paper, Paolini–Shelah, works in the language of groups; the graph coding of function symbols exists only syntactically), joint continuity of the action, and López-Escobar. The last two are targets of the merged roadmap [CountableStructureSpaces](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/main/TauCetiRoadmap/CountableStructureSpaces/README.md) (§1.2 `ContinuousSMul` from `continuous_uncurry_restrict`, Layer 5 `lopezEscobar_iff`), which is written over the Tau Ceti coding space and keeps decoding relational; open PR #2418 §1.3 specifies the functional coding space for languages with function symbols (Polish, continuous `Equiv.Perm ℕ` action, orbits the isomorphism classes) and its continuous equivariant embedding into the graph-language coding space.

7. López-Escobar (98.5): CountableStructureSpaces Layer 5.
